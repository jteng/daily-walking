## SHOULD-FIX

### S1. The 1:5 present-tense "he loves us" is used a third time; the meditation also quotes 1:5 twice
- **Location:** 默想 / Meditation, ¶3 and ¶4.
  - ¶3 zh 「约翰在第5节就说了：「他爱我们，用自己的血使我们脱离罪恶」。」 / en "John said it in verse 5: \"To him who loves us and has freed us from our sins by his blood.\""
  - ¶4 zh 「可是约翰写的「他爱我们」，原文是现在式：不是从前爱过，等我失败了就收回，而是现在还爱着。」 / en "But John writes \"who loves us,\" in the present tense: not a love he once had…"
- **Problem:** The 1:5 praise is already the pivot of 00's meditation ("he goes on loving us now"). The present-tense argument is also the hinge of 02's meditation ("'loves' is present tense: he loves us still"). The brief lists the 1:5 praise as used. Three chapters in a row would rest the reader who has failed on the same verb-tense point. The 1:5 exegesis can stay in the 01 body (§1:4-8 pt 4), where it belongs. In the meditation, the word to the repeat-failer can come from John's own story instead. Mark 9:38 fits this scenario's envy of another's ministry exactly, and it is unused in the plan.
- **Fix:**
  - ¶3: delete the sentence quoted above. The sentence before it already gives the gospel ground: 「他所看见的每一样，他都已经在十字架上担当了」 / "everything he sees, he has already borne on the cross."
  - ¶4: replace the whole paragraph.
    - **Old zh:** 「也许我这样认过许多次，又一再落回同样的轻看和嫉妒里，觉得没有脸再到他面前。可是约翰写的「他爱我们」，原文是现在式：不是从前爱过，等我失败了就收回，而是现在还爱着。他看见我一再跌倒，也看见他自己为我流的血。」
    - **New zh:** 「也许我这样认过许多次，又一再落回同样的轻看和嫉妒里，觉得没有脸再到他面前。约翰也有过这样的心。年轻的时候，他看见有人奉耶稣的名赶鬼，就去禁止，因为那人「不跟从我们」（可9:38）；他和哥哥还求主，将来在荣耀里让他们一个坐在右边，一个坐在左边（可10:37）。主纠正过他，却没有丢下他。几十年过去，仆倒在拔摩岛上的，还是这个约翰；主对他说的，仍是「不要惧怕」。主没有等他把这些都改好了才这样说，他凭的是自己为约翰流的血。他待我也是这样。」
    - **Old en:** "Perhaps I have confessed like this many times and keep slipping back into the same contempt and envy, and feel I have no face left to come before him. But John writes \"who loves us,\" in the present tense: not a love he once had and withdraws when I fail, but a love he has now. He sees me fall again and again, and he also sees the blood he shed for me."
    - **New en:** "Perhaps I have confessed like this many times and keep slipping back into the same contempt and envy, and feel I have no face left to come before him. John knew that heart too. As a young man he saw someone casting out demons in Jesus' name and tried to stop him, \"because he was not following us\" (Mark 9:38); he and his brother asked to sit at Jesus' right and left in his glory (Mark 10:37). The Lord corrected him, but he did not let him go. Decades later the man lying on the ground on Patmos is the same John, and the Lord's word to him is still \"Fear not.\" The Lord did not wait for John to put all this right before he said it; he spoke on the ground of the blood he had shed for John. He deals with me the same way."
  - (Mark 9:38 CUV is quoted verbatim. Mark 10:37 is paraphrased, so it has no 「」.) After this fix, 02 can keep its present-tense point as the plan's single use.

### S2. The ending leans on a door and on "shutting out", which are used and retired images
- **Location:** 默想 / Meditation, last paragraph: 「有一天站在他面前，它们会把我关在门外……门开不开，不在我心里那些暗处，而在他。有一天，他要开门，叫在他里面的人出来」 / "they will shut me out… Whether the door opens… One day he will open it…"
- **Problem:**
  - Door endings are already used: 2 Peter 00 ends at the door of the eternal kingdom, and the brief keeps the 3:20 door away from 03.
  - "Shut out / standing outside" is a retired frame (1 Cor 11–12, Phil 3, Col 2).
  - The brief asks for an ending on the keys. Here the keys lead into a door, and the door takes over the final image.
  - Keep it off fear of death as such, which is Hebrews 02's meditation (that meditation also quotes the keys).
- **Fix (last paragraph):**
  - **Old zh:** 「我心底最怕的，是这些事会有最后的话：有一天站在他面前，它们会把我关在门外。可是主在这里说的最后一句话，正是对着这个怕说的：他「拿着死亡和阴间的钥匙」。看透我的那双眼睛，和拿着钥匙的，是同一位为我死过、现在又活了的主。门开不开，不在我心里那些暗处，而在他。有一天，他要开门，叫在他里面的人出来，从此「不再有死亡」（21:4）。钥匙在他那里。」
  - **New zh:** 「我心底最怕的，是这些事会有最后的话：有一天站在他面前，它们要定我的罪。可是主在这里说的最后一句话，正是对着这个怕说的：他「拿着死亡和阴间的钥匙」。看透我的那双眼睛，和拿着钥匙的，是同一位为我死过、现在又活了的主。他已经替我进到死里去，又从那里出来了；死亡和阴间的锁开不开，不在我心里那些暗处，而在他。有一天，他要用这钥匙，叫属他的人从死里出来，从此「不再有死亡」（21:4）。钥匙在他那里。」
  - **Old en:** "My deepest fear is that these things will have the last word: that one day, standing before him, they will shut me out. … Whether the door opens does not rest with the dark places in my heart; it rests with him. One day he will open it and bring out those who are in him, and \"death shall be no more\" (21:4). The keys are with him."
  - **New en:** "My deepest fear is that these things will have the last word: that one day, standing before him, they will condemn me. But the last thing the Lord says here speaks to exactly that fear: he has \"the keys of Death and Hades.\" The eyes that see through me and the one who holds the keys are the same Lord, who died for me and is alive. He has gone down into death in my place and come out again; whether the lock of Death and Hades opens does not rest with the dark places in my heart, but with him. One day he will use those keys to bring his own out of death, and \"death shall be no more\" (21:4). The keys are with him."

## CONSIDER

### C1. A word for the reader who does not yet trust him
- **Location:** ¶3, 「凡投靠他的人，那双看透我们的眼睛，是一位为我们死过的主的眼睛」 / "For all who trust him, the eyes that see through us…"
- **Problem:** The qualifier is right, but the doubter or newcomer is left standing outside it. Avoid the stock "If I have never trusted him, today I can…" shape (Hebrews 00/02).
- **Fix:** After that sentence add:
  - zh 「我若还不曾投靠他，这句「不要惧怕」也是向我说的邀请：不是叫我先把自己收拾好，而是叫我就这样到他那里去。」
  - en "If I have not yet trusted him, this \"Fear not\" is an invitation to me as well: not to tidy myself up first, but to come to him as I am."

### C2. The examples lean toward church workers
- **Location:** ¶1: 「朋友的事工兴旺、常被请去讲道」 / "a friend whose ministry flourishes and who is often invited to preach".
- **Problem:** Together with the co-worker and the prayer meeting, all three examples assume ministry roles. A newer believer may not see themselves.
- **Fix:** zh 「团契里一位朋友常被大家称赞、请去带领」 / en "a friend in the fellowship whom everyone praises and keeps asking to lead". Optional.

### C3. English wording
- **Location:** ¶1, "No one can audit these things".
- **Problem:** "Audit" reads oddly in English.
- **Fix:** "No one else can see these things". The zh 「没有人查得出来」 is fine.

### C4. Something to delight in
- **Problem:** The meditation moves from fear to relief, which is right for 1:17. The glory of the Son of Man (face like the sun, voice like many waters) stays in the body, and the reader is not invited to admire him for himself.
- **Fix:** No change required. If the Chinese pass trims anything, keep the 「不要惧怕」 grounded in 「我是首先的，我是末后的」 (who he is) and do not shrink it to "my sins are covered".

## What works and should be kept
- The assigned shape is carried out faithfully: holy fear (1:17), the flaming eyes tied to 2:23, "Fear not" grounded entirely in who Christ is (1:17-18), and the ending on the keys with no hand image.
- ¶2's two-sided address is pastorally sharp: "never been afraid → made the Lord too small" and "only want to hide → not yet heard what he says next".
- The heart sins are specific, honest and fit the eyes that search "mind and heart": contempt that discounts a co-worker, congratulating aloud while souring inside, prayers performed for an audience. The Sunday-school-picture opening is fresh in the plan.
- Indicative comes before imperative. The confession and the apology to the co-worker come after the cross, and the restitution step is kept to half a sentence.
- In the body, the `> 要点` lines for 1:4-8 ("everything in Revelation is written to these beloved people"), 1:12-16 ("the light is not its own") and 1:17-20 are grace-first and Christ-centered. The martyrs line in §1:17-20 pt 4 roots faithfulness in the keys, not in their own strength. The note "ask where the OT used an image before which news story it matches" is a good pastoral guard.
