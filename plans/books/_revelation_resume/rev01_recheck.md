## Remaining findings

**R1 (should-fix). The church-ages point is fair but not quite symmetric.**
- **Location:** 深读 1:9-11 point 5 / Going Deeper 1:9–11 point 5.
- **Problem:** The second reading gets reasons, and those reasons argue against the first: 「经文本身没有指出先后的时期，每封信又都是「向众教会」说的」 / "the text itself marks no succession of periods…". The first reading is described but gets no reason of its own.
- **Fix:** Give the first reading one clause of its own case.
  - zh, after 「……从使徒时代的以弗所，到末后冷淡的老底嘉。」 insert: 「持这看法的人认为，1:20把这些称为「奥秘」，而七封信的次序又和教会历史的大致走向相合。」 The quote 「奥秘」 is CUV (1:20).
  - en, after "…from apostolic Ephesus to lukewarm Laodicea at the end." insert: "Its holders point to the word 'mystery' (1:20) and to how the order of the letters seems to match the broad course of church history."

**C1 (consider, optional). An OT background for the stars.** It is not required, because 1:20 interprets the stars itself. If one is wanted, add it after 「他右手拿着七星，就是七个教会的使者（1:20）」 / "the angels of the seven churches (1:20)":
- zh: 「（旧约有时用星比喻天上的使者，也比喻神忠心的百姓：「晨星一同歌唱，神的众子也都欢呼」，伯38:7；「那使多人归义的，必发光如星」，但12:3）」
- en: "(the Old Testament can use stars for heavenly beings and for God's faithful people: 'the morning stars sang together and all the sons of God shouted for joy,' Job 38:7; 'those who turn many to righteousness, like the stars,' Dan 12:3)"

Both quotes were checked against CUV and ESV.

**C2 (consider). The citation for "faithful to death."**
- **Location:** 深读 1:4-8 point 3: 「那忠心到死（提前6:13）」 / "faithful to death (1 Tim 6:13)".
- **Problem:** 1 Tim 6:13 is about Jesus' good confession before Pilate, so it supports "faithful witness," not "to death."
- **Fix:**
  - zh: 「那在彼拉多面前作过美好见证（提前6:13）、忠心到死」
  - en: "who made the good confession before Pilate (1 Tim 6:13) and was faithful to death"
