1 John 04's meditation (WORKFLOW: "a cold, divided love for God… loving God's gifts more than God") is built on 「我们爱，因为神先爱我们」 (4:19), 「爱是回应」 and 「悔改不是逼自己产生感觉」. 02 ¶5 quotes the same verse and argues the same thing. Keep 1:5 (now 02's alone) and swap 4:19 for John's own Gospel, John 13:1, which is not used in any meditation (1 Cor 13 has it only in the body).

zh new: 写下这话的约翰，在他的福音书里也记下主怎样爱门徒：「他既然爱世间属自己的人，就爱他们到底。」（约13:1）所以，「起初的爱」最先不是我的感觉，而是他的爱；我的爱从来只是回应。

## CONSIDER

### 4. 02 meditation, final sentence: make the gift God-centred and tie it back to the "old ox"
The ending is right (the tree of life), but at the moment the reward is only "something to eat". Tying it to the end of the Ephesian toil (2:2) closes the busy-service theme with rest and gift rather than wages. It avoids the throne (04/05 end there) and avoids hands.
zh old: 有一天，他要把神乐园中生命树的果子赐给我吃。
zh new: 有一天，我一切的劳碌都要止息，他要亲自把神乐园中生命树的果子赐给我吃。
en old: and one day he will give me the fruit of the tree of life in the paradise of God.
en new: and one day, when all my toil is over, he himself will give me the fruit of the tree of life in the paradise of God.

## WHAT WORKS (keep)
- 02 meditation: the "old ox" scenario is concrete and honest: Bible reading only to prepare lessons, praying only when it is my turn, resentment of those who don't help. The word for the one who never "felt" first love is the best pastoral move in either chapter. "I know" is read as seeing the labor, not keeping accounts. Burnout is separated from sin (share the load, pause a ministry), while coldness and resentment are still named as sin. The flaming sword of Gen 3:24, borne by Christ, opens the way back to the tree.
- 02 body: every 要点 ends on what Christ gives (his love, the crown of life, manna and a new name, himself as the morning star). The Thyatira safeguarding section and the faithful-pastor balance are good, and so is the Jezebel-label caution. The synagogue-of-Satan care is good.
- 03 meditation: "this is about rich people, not me". Self-reliance as a cultural virtue that quietly becomes self-sufficiency before God, which is idol-level diagnosis rather than behaviour. "Not realizing" as the deepest poverty. 「我买他的金子，付款的是他」 is excellent, gospel-shaped and memorable; keep it exactly. The small step (praying "I need you… give me salve") needs no spiritual record, and the ending on 3:19 lands on being loved.
- 03 body: Philadelphia's "little power" point for small churches; "in a dead church the Lord still knows a few names"; Isa 55:1 "buy without money" as grace; 3:19 as a father's love; 3:20 kept in its church context.
