## Consider

### 3. 04 Meditation ¶4: God is only a refuge here, never someone to delight in
Problem: The chapter's own point (4:9-11 §3) is that God is worthy "not because he meets our needs." Yet the meditation uses the throne only to calm the reader. One sentence lets the reader worship as well as be calmed. It also turns the closing "I can lift my eyes" into adoration.
zh old: 「但我所面对的这一切，都在这个宝座之下。」
zh new: 「但我所面对的这一切，都在这个宝座之下。四活物和长老昼夜敬拜，不是因为地上的局势好转了，而是因为坐在宝座上的那一位配得；他不只是我慌乱时的避难所，他本身就值得我仰望。」
en old: "But all that I face lies beneath this throne."
en new: "But all that I face lies beneath this throne. The living creatures and the elders worship day and night, not because things on earth have improved, but because the One on the throne is worthy; he is not only my refuge when I panic, he is himself worth looking at."

### 4. 04 Meditation ¶2: comparing whose suffering is worse
Problem: "More out of control than ours" ranks suffering. For a reader whose relative is in a war zone (the meditation's own example) it can sound dismissive.
zh old: 「约翰在拔摩海岛上，看见的世界比我们更失控。」
zh new: 「约翰在拔摩海岛上，所面对的世界一点也不比我们的安稳。」
en old: "On the island of Patmos John saw a world more out of control than ours."
en new: "On the island of Patmos John faced a world no steadier than ours."

### 5. 04 Meditation ¶3: abrupt switch from "he" to "I"
zh old: 「在慌乱中，我也许已经做了许多不好的事：」
zh new: 「这位弟兄心里的那个声音，我也很熟悉。在慌乱中，我也许已经做了许多不好的事：」
en old: "In my panic I may already have done many wrong things:"
en new: "I know that voice too. In my panic I may already have done many wrong things:"

### 6. 04 Meditation ¶3 uses 05's ending image too early
Problem: 05's meditation ends on the Lamb standing "at the very center of the throne." 04 uses the same picture mid-meditation, which takes some of the force from 05's ending.
zh old: 「在第5章，约翰要看见那宝座中间站着一只羔羊，像是被杀过的；」
zh new: 「在第5章，约翰要看见一只羔羊，像是被杀过的；」
en old: "In chapter 5 John will see a Lamb standing at the center of that throne, as though it had been slain;"
en new: "In chapter 5 John will see a Lamb, as though it had been slain;"

### 7. 05 Meditation ¶2: two different elders, both called 长老
Problem: ¶1 ends with the church's elder weeping. ¶2 opens 「长老对约翰说」, which at first reads as that same man. Naming the heavenly elder also makes a good link: heaven's elder speaks to earth's weeping elder.
zh old: 「长老对约翰说：「不要哭！看哪，犹大支派中的狮子」。」
zh new: 「天上那位长老对约翰说：「不要哭！看哪，犹大支派中的狮子」。」
en old: "The elder said to John, "Weep no more; behold, the Lion.""
en new: "The elder in heaven said to John, "Weep no more; behold, the Lion.""

### 8. 05 Meditation ¶4: the chapter climaxes in song, but the meditation has none
Problem: The response is to pray, support the preacher and know who holds the scroll. All good, but the reader is never invited into the worship the chapter is building to. Quote 5:12, not 5:9 (5:9 "every tribe" is Titus's).
zh old: 「也可以一起扶持那位累坏了的传道人。」
zh new: 「也可以一起扶持那位累坏了的传道人。在那间空了一半的客厅里，我们还可以和天上千千万万的声音一同唱：「曾被杀的羔羊是配得权柄、丰富、智慧、能力、尊贵、荣耀、颂赞的」（5:12）。这首歌，不必等到局面翻转了才开始唱。」
en old: "we can hold up the exhausted preacher together."
en new: "we can hold up the exhausted preacher together. In that half-empty living room we can also join the myriads of heaven and sing, "Worthy is the Lamb who was slain, to receive power and wealth and wisdom and might and honor and glory and blessing!" (5:12). That song does not have to wait until things turn around."

## Cross-chapter notes (outside 04-05; for the 01-03 application reviews)
- Rev 1:5 「他爱我们」 carries the gospel turn in the meditations of 00, 01 and 02 (01 and 02 both press the present tense). Consider moving one of 01/02 to a different anchor.
- Rev 7:14 (robes washed in the Lamb's blood) is quoted in both the 00 and 03 meditations.
- 04 and 05 avoid both, and avoid the hand, door, doxology and "came near" endings. Good.
- 05's small church in decline (young families moving away) brushes against 2 Timothy 02 (church decline), but the pivot differs (Lion and Lamb, not "the word is not bound"). Acceptable.

## What works and should be kept
- 04: the gospel comes before any step: 「我不需要先让自己平静下来，才可以来到这个宝座前；正是在我不平静的时候，我被请到这里来看。」 An excellent word for the anxious reader who has failed.
- 04: it names honestly both anxiety and resignation (「焦虑和认命都不是信心」) without crushing the reader. The ordinary duties (accounts, asking for help and prayer) come after the throne, not instead of it.
- 04: the rainbow ending as assigned. It ties Gen 9:16 to the Lamb's blood (「记念这约的，是神自己，不是我」), so God's covenant faithfulness, not the reader's grip, holds the ending.
- 04 points: 4:6b-8 (holiness makes grace wonderful, not cheap) and 4:11 §3 ("not because he meets our needs") are God-centered and keep the chapter from turning into therapy.
- 05: wanting a lion. The prayers in the scene (repayment, every door flung open) are recognisable and are not mocked. It affirms that prayer matters (5:8) and that judgment is real, then shows his power in the slain Lamb.
- 05: it is honest about the hidden sins of a church under pressure (complaining about the preacher, planning an exit, wanting the other side to fall), and immediately says 「羔羊所流的血，正是为这样的人流的」. Praying for those who trouble the church grows out of the Lamb who bled for his enemies. That is grace, not moralism.
- 05 body: "the cross is his qualification" and the Eastern Lightning paragraph with its pastor/police line.
- 05: the ending on the slain-yet-standing Lamb is assigned, fresh and Christ-centered.
