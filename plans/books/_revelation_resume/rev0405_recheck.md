## Remaining (consider only; not blocking)
A. 05 5:1-4 §2, Bauckham contrast. The current wording 「包衡则认为…」 / "Bauckham argues instead that…" can suggest Beale denies that the scrolls of ch 10 and ch 5 are the same. As far as I recall, Beale (NIGTC) also identifies them. Bauckham's distinctive claim is that the seals are preliminary.
   - zh old: 「包衡则认为，第10章那「展开」的小书卷（10:2）就是这卷书：七印只是预备，」
   - zh new: 「包衡认为，第10章那「展开」的小书卷（10:2）就是这卷书；他特别的地方在于主张七印只是预备，」
   - en old: "Bauckham argues instead that the "little scroll open in his hand" (10:2) is this same scroll: the seals are preliminary, and"
   - en new: "Bauckham identifies the "little scroll open in his hand" (10:2) with this same scroll, and argues distinctively that the seals are preliminary and that"
B. Meditation overlap between 04 and 05, for the application reviewer to weigh. Both scenes have a church losing its meeting place (04: the landlord does not renew the lease; 05: the meeting place moved three times).
   - Suggest replacing the 04 detail.
   - zh old: 「他们教会租用多年的聚会场所，房东忽然说不再续租了，弟兄姊妹只好分散在几个家里聚会。」
   - zh new: 「他的女儿在外地读书，最近说不想再去教会了。」
   - The later line 「下个主日在谁家聚会也还要安排」 then needs to change too, e.g. to 「女儿那里我也还要慢慢地说」.
   - en: change to "His daughter, studying in another city, has recently said she no longer wants to go to church." and "still find words for my daughter".
