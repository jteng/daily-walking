## 06.md

### 06-S1 (should-fix): surname clash, Chen used in four other Revelation meditations
- **Location:** 默想 / Meditation, paragraphs 1, 3, 4: 「老陈每天早上七点出门……」「老陈不能」「对老陈来说」 / "Mr. Chen" (three times).
- **Problem:** Chen is already 陈姊妹 (10), 小陈 (13), 陈弟兄 (15) and 陈姊妹 (17). Five Chens across the book make the scenarios blur together. English also says "Mr. Chen" while 07 and 09 use "Lao Liu" and "Lao Zheng".
- **Rewrite:** Change every 老陈 to **老冯**, and every "Mr. Chen" to **"Lao Feng"**. No other Revelation 00-22 meditation uses 冯.

### 06-S2 (should-fix): the river scene needs a word for the reader who is thinking of ending it all
- **Location:** 默想 paragraph 1 「他每天把车开到河边，在车里一坐就是一天」, and paragraph 4 (the practical steps).
- **Problem:** A man hiding gambling debts, sitting alone by a river every day and afraid to speak, is in a high-risk situation for suicide, and the reader who recognises himself may be too. The meditation gives good steps (tell the wife with a pastor present, recovery help, police for violent collectors, a word for the wife) but says nothing to the reader who has started to think his family would be better off without him. 2 Corinthians 01 set the precedent with a crisis-help line.
- **Rewrite:** In paragraph 4, after the police sentence, insert:
  - zh: 如果你像他一样，坐在河边的时候曾经想过「不如一了百了」，「家里没有我反而好」，请今天就告诉一个人：你的配偶、牧者、一位可信的朋友，或者打当地的心理危机热线。债可以慢慢还，你的命不能拿来抵债；羔羊为你流的血，不是要你再拿自己的命去还。
  - en: If, like him, you have sat by the river and thought, "Better to end it all," or "My family would be better off without me," tell someone today: your spouse, a pastor, a trusted friend, or a local crisis line. Debts can be repaid slowly; your life is not payment for them. The Lamb shed his blood for you; he does not ask you to pay with your own.

### 06-C1 (consider): use Luke 23:30 instead of Heb 13:12 for the closing turn
- **Location:** 默想 last paragraph: 「可是有一位，已经在城门外为我们倒下了（来13:12）。」 / "But One has already fallen for us, outside the city gate (Heb 13:12)."
- **Problem:** Heb 13:12 is already in the 2 Peter 02 meditation (and Hebrews 13's outside-the-camp scene), and 07's meditation also says the Lamb was 「在城外被杀」, so the two neighbouring chapters close on the same image. "Fallen for us" is also a weak word for the cross in English, where "fallen" suggests sin or defeat. The chapter's own body (§6:16-17, point 3) supplies a stronger, unused link: Jesus quoted "fall on us" on his way to the cross (Luke 23:30). It also lets the last paragraph name Lao Feng's own hiding-phrase, so the reader is not left saying "I sat in a car by the river" when they never did.
- **Rewrite (whole last paragraph):**
  - zh: 那一天，许多人要向山喊：「倒在我们身上吧！」这句话，主耶稣早就说过，是在他背着十字架出城的路上（路23:30）。他没有去找山洞，而是一直走到各各他，让本该落在我们身上的忿怒落在他自己身上。那位曾被杀的羔羊，从死里复活，现在站在宝座中间（5:6）。所以老冯不必再躲在河边的车里，不必躲在教会的后排，也不必躲在「还行，就是忙」这句话后面；我也不必再躲在我的那一句话后面。主啊，我从我的藏身之处出来；「你是我藏身之处」（诗32:7），求你作我惟一的藏身之处。
  - en: On that day many will cry to the mountains, "Fall on us." Jesus spoke those words long before, on the road out of the city with his cross (Luke 23:30). He did not look for a cave; he walked on to Golgotha and let the wrath that should have fallen on us fall on himself. The Lamb who was slain rose from the dead and now stands at the center of the throne (5:6). So Lao Feng no longer needs to hide in a car by the river, or in the back row of the church, or behind "It's fine, just busy"; and I no longer need to hide behind my own version of that sentence. Lord, I come out from my hiding place; "You are a hiding place for me" (Ps 32:7); be my only hiding place.

### 06-C2 (consider): give the reader who is not a gambler a heart question
- **Location:** 默想 paragraph 2: 「我们躲的不只是家人的眼光，更是那一张脸。」 / "What we hide from is not only our family's eyes; it is that face."
- **Problem:** Apart from the general "we", the reader watches Lao Feng from outside. One specific, answerable question would reach the reader's own hiding and motives (shame, fear of losing face) without a retired frame (no phone, no accusing voice).
- **Rewrite:** after that sentence insert:
  - zh: 我们各人也有自己的河边：一笔没告诉配偶的开销，一件瞒了父母多年的事，一个一再重犯、从来不敢说出口的罪。我在躲的是什么？我怕的，是丢脸，还是那一张脸？
  - en: Each of us has a riverside of our own: money spent that a spouse was never told about, something kept from our parents for years, a sin repeated again and again and never once said aloud. What am I hiding? And what do I fear more: losing face, or that face?

### 06-C3 (consider): the closed small factory repeats 04's scenario
- **Location:** 默想 paragraph 1: 「跟妻子说去厂里。其实那家小厂半年前就关了。他先是向亲戚借钱发工资」 / "tells his wife he is going to the factory. In fact the small factory closed six months ago. First he borrowed from relatives to pay the workers' wages".
- **Problem:** Revelation 04's meditation opens on a brother whose small factory has lost half its orders. A different failed business keeps the two apart.
- **Rewrite:**
  - zh: 跟妻子说去公司。其实他那家小装修公司半年前就关了。他先是向亲戚借钱给工人结工钱
  - en: tells his wife he is going to the office. In fact his small renovation company closed six months ago. First he borrowed from relatives to pay the workers what he owed them

Note (no change needed): 2 Cor 5:21 is assigned by the brief but is already in the 2 John 00, 2 Peter 02 and Philemon 00 meditations. It is the right verse for the wrath-bearing Lamb, so keep it, but list it in WORKFLOW as used. Gen 3:9 "Where are you?" and Ps 32:7 are fresh.

## 07.md

### 07-S1 (should-fix): the closing promise is applied to the whole family without condition
- **Location:** 默想 last paragraph: 「最后擦去眼泪的那一位，是神自己……亲自为他们擦去。到那日，宝座中的羔羊要领他们到生命水的泉源」 / "the God who has kept count of every tear, wiping them away himself. On that day the Lamb in the midst of the throne will guide them".
- **Problem:** In 7:14-17 the promise is for those washed in the Lamb's blood. The meditation says Lao Liu believed, but not the mother, the wife or the son, and the previous paragraph is rightly careful about the dead whose faith is unknown. Saying 7:17 will be fulfilled for "this family" without condition blurs the gospel call. It should become an invitation.
- **Rewrite (replace the last two sentences of the paragraph):**
  - zh: 这个应许是给一切投靠羔羊的人的：给已经站在宝座前的老宋，也给还坐在门口等的母亲、还在操心赔偿的妻子、还把自己关在屋里的儿子，只要他们也到这位为羊舍命的牧人这里来。到那日，宝座中的羔羊要领他的羊到生命水的泉源；「神也必擦去他们一切的眼泪」（7:17）。
  - en: This promise belongs to all who take refuge in the Lamb: to Lao Song, already before the throne, and to the mother still waiting at the door, the wife still dealing with the compensation, the son still shut in his room, if they too come to this shepherd who laid down his life for the sheep. On that day the Lamb in the midst of the throne will guide his sheep to springs of living water, "and God will wipe away every tear from their eyes" (7:17).

### 07-S2 (should-fix): the son's last words are treated only as sin to confess
- **Location:** 默想 paragraph 4: 「他可以把那句话带到羔羊面前，承认它，哭出来；凡信靠他的，他的罪羔羊已经担当了。」 / "The son can bring those words to the Lamb, confess them and weep; for all who trust him, the Lamb has already carried their sin."
- **Problem:** "You never cared about me" from a junior-high boy whose father was home once a year is sharp, but it is also years of real hurt (the 留守儿童 experience many Chinese readers share). If it is framed only as guilt to confess, a grieving teenager, or an adult who was once that child, is told that his pain is his sin. The gospel word for his sharpness should come alongside an acknowledgment of his loss, and his not crying should not be read as hardness.
- **Rewrite (replace the first four sentences of the paragraph):**
  - zh: 那个儿子心里也许最难过的，是那最后一句话。他收不回来了。那句话里有气，也有这些年一年只见一次爸爸的苦；他一滴眼泪都没流，不是因为他不在乎。他可以把这两样都带到羔羊面前：把那句伤人的话照实承认，也把多年的想念和委屈说出来。凡信靠这位牧人的，他的罪羔羊已经担当了，他的苦羔羊也都知道。
  - en: What may hurt the son most is that last sentence. He cannot take it back. There was anger in it, and also the pain of years of seeing his father once a year; that he has not cried does not mean he does not care. He can bring both to the Lamb: confess the words that wounded, and also say out loud the years of missing him and feeling left behind. For all who trust this shepherd, the Lamb has already carried their sin, and he knows their sorrow too.

### 07-S3 (should-fix): surname clash with 11's Xiao Liu
- **Location:** 默想 throughout: 「老刘」 / "Lao Liu".
- **Problem:** 11's meditation is built on 小刘 / Xiao Liu, so two of five neighbouring chapters use Liu (and 12/21 already share 秀兰, which should be flagged to those chapters' reviewers).
- **Rewrite:** Change every 老刘 to **老宋**, and every "Lao Liu" to **"Lao Song"**. This is used in 07-S1 above.

### 07-C1 (consider): the rest sentence overlaps with 14's assigned theme
- **Location:** 默想 paragraph 3: 「他一辈子没有机会好好休息；现在牧养他的，是宝座中的羔羊。」 / "He never had the chance to rest properly; now the one who shepherds him is the Lamb in the midst of the throne."
- **Problem:** "A life of toil, now at rest" is exactly 14's meditation (Mother Zheng, "rest from their labors"). 7:16's "hunger no more" fits a migrant worker just as well and keeps the two apart.
- **Rewrite:**
  - zh: 他一辈子舍不得吃，好一点的饭菜都省下来，把钱寄回家；经上却说，他们「不再饥，不再渴」，现在牧养他的，是宝座中的羔羊。
  - en: All his life he went without, skipping the better meals to send money home; yet Scripture says they "shall hunger no more, neither thirst anymore," and now the one who shepherds him is the Lamb in the midst of the throne.

### 07-C2 (consider): turn once to the reader's own grief
- **Location:** 默想 paragraph 2, after 「神是公义的神。」 / "God is a God of justice."
- **Problem:** The whole meditation is about one family. Most readers are not migrant workers' families but carry a death of their own. One sentence lets them step in without a task.
- **Rewrite:** insert:
  - zh: 也许你心里也有这样一场丧事：一句没来得及说的对不起，一位还在等的老人，一份到今天还哭不出来的难过。这段经文也是写给你的。
  - en: Perhaps you carry a death like this too: an apology you never got to say, an old parent still waiting, a grief you still cannot cry. This passage is written for you as well.

Note: 07's "slain outside the city" (paragraph 3) can stay if 06-C1 removes Heb 13:12 from 06. Ps 56:8 and John 10:11 are fresh in meditations; John 1:14 is used in several earlier books but here it is tied to 7:15's tent verb and works.

## What works well (keep)

- **06:** It is pastorally right to hide from the face and not only from family (6:16; Gen 3:8), and God's first word is a question, not a sentence (Gen 3:9). The paradox "hiding in him is coming out into the light" is the heart of the gospel and lands. The order of grace before steps is right: substitution is stated plainly (2 Cor 5:21) before any step, and there is a word for the one who relapsed ("the Lamb's blood has not lost its power") and a careful, specific word for the wife (no secret-keeping, no signing for his debts, safety first, "his wrong is not your fault"). The 要点 lines keep God at the center ("The Lamb has not lost his hold on history"; "those hidden in the Lamb"), and the 6→7 link (caves → standing before the throne) is excellent.
- **07:** The scenario is honest, current and concrete (heatstroke on scaffolding, ashes carried home, compensation talks), and the meditation refuses to hurry the grieving ("We need not hurry to tell this family not to cry"). Justice is affirmed (fair compensation, legal aid), and the line on loved ones whose faith is unknown is careful. Each clause of 7:15-16 is mapped onto Lao Liu's life (sun, site dormitories, the tent), so the promise feels like comfort and not like information. The ending is God-centered ("not time... not friends... but God himself") and closes on 7:17 as assigned, without a task. In the body, the white robes "not woven by themselves" and the cry "Salvation belongs to our God... and to the Lamb" keep the glory with God.
