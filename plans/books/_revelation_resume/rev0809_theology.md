## Rulings on the coordinator's cross-chapter questions

**(1) Structure duplicated in 08 §8:1-2 point 2.** Agreed. 06.md already sets out all three readings once for the book, naming Victorinus, Hendriksen and Beale; Walvoord; and Ladd and Osborne. 08 repeats that survey almost word for word. **(2) "Leans idealist" vs 00's eclectic reading.** Agreed. This adopts the idealist school against 00.md's stated eclectic approach (00 zh l.86, en l.196). Replacement is in M2. 09's matching sentence (§9:7-12 point 3) is already eclectic and can stay.

**(3) 老陈 in both 06 and 09.** Rename the man in 09 to **老周 / Lao Zhou**. No Revelation chapter uses that name. Change all 4 zh occurrences (「老陈」 in paragraphs 1, 3, 4) and all 3 en occurrences ("Lao Chen").

**Suggested fix (replace the whole point):**
- zh: 「**2. 七号和七印的关系。** 七印、七号、七碗是先后发生还是重复叙述，第6章已经把几种读法摆出来了（见第6章「七印和后面的七号、七碗是什么关系？」）。这里只补充两点和七号直接有关的地方。第一，第七印揭开以后，除了寂静，并没有自己的灾难，接着就是七位天使领受七枝号（8:1-2）；所以主张先后发生的学者（例如约翰·华尔伏）说，七号就是第七印的内容，主张重复叙述的学者（例如毕尔）则认为，寂静本身就是第一组「七」的终局，七号再从头讲起。第二，两种读法都看到灾难越来越重：印的灾难临到「四分之一」（6:8），号的灾难临到「三分之一」，碗的灾难却没有限度。」

### M3. 08.md §深读 8:1-2, point 4 (and 本章脉络 / The Point): the purpose of the trumpets is a disputed view stated as fact
**Location:** 「所以号声同时是审判的宣告和悔改的警告。前六枝号的灾难都有限度，正是为了叫人在结局以前听见、回转。」 / "So the trumpet is both an announcement of judgment and a warning to repent. The plagues of the first six trumpets are all limited, precisely so that people may hear and turn before the end." Also in §8:7-12 point 2: 「神在审判中仍然留着余地，好叫人还有机会回转。」 / "In judgment God still holds back, so that people still have room to turn."
**Problem:** Whether the trumpets aim at repentance is debated. Bauckham and Osborne, among others, read them as warning judgments; the warning judgments fail (9:20-21), and only the church's witness brings people to give glory (11:13). Beale argues the reverse: following the Exodus plagues, which hardened Pharaoh, the trumpets are mainly punitive judgments that expose and confirm hardness, not primarily calls to repentance. The text supports both readings: 9:20 says "did not repent", which implies that repentance was the fitting response, yet the plagues are modelled on hardening plagues. 09 builds its meditation on this point, so it needs to be stated fairly once.
**Suggested fix (08 §8:1-2 point 4 closing):**
- zh: 「所以号声是审判的宣告，在旧约中也常常是警告。释经者对七号的目的看法不同：许多人（例如包衡、格兰特·奥斯本）认为，前六枝号的灾难有限度，是为了叫人在结局以前听见、回转；毕尔则强调，正像埃及的十灾使法老越发刚硬，这些审判主要是显明、也定准了人心的刚硬。第9章末了「仍旧不悔改」（9:20-21）这句话，两种读法都要认真对待。」
- en: "So the trumpet announces judgment, and in the Old Testament it is often a warning too. Interpreters differ on the trumpets' purpose: many (e.g. Bauckham, Grant Osborne) take the limited plagues of the first six trumpets as meant to make people hear and turn before the end; Beale stresses that, as the plagues of Egypt hardened Pharaoh, these judgments chiefly expose and confirm hardened hearts. Both readings have to reckon with the closing words of chapter 9, "they did not repent" (9:20–21)."
In §8:7-12 point 2, soften to: 「神在审判中仍然有所保留」 / "In judgment God still holds back" (delete the purpose clause, or add 「许多释经者认为，这是要给人回转的余地」 / "many take this as leaving room to turn").

### M4. 09.md §深读 9:13-19, point 3: historicist arithmetic misattributed
**Location:** 「历史派曾把一时、一日、一月、一年按一日顶一年的算法算成约三百九十一年，读作从土耳其人兴起到1453年攻陷君士坦丁堡」 / "Historicists once counted 'an hour, a day, a month and a year,' by the day-for-a-year principle, as about 391 years, reading it as the period from the rise of the Turks to the fall of Constantinople in 1453".
**Problem:** As stated, this is a factual error. Elliott's figure for the Turks-to-1453 reading was about 396 years (365-day year, from c. 1057). The 391-year figure (360-day year) is chiefly Josiah Litch's, running to 1840. The draft also leaves out the most useful exegetical point: in Greek a single article governs all four nouns (*eis tēn hōran kai hēmeran kai mēna kai eniauton*). That points to one appointed moment, not a span of time to be added up. Beale and Osborne make this point.
**Suggested fix:**
- zh: 「历史派（例如十九世纪的艾略特）曾按一日顶一年的算法，把一时、一日、一月、一年加起来，算成约三百九十六年，读作从十一世纪土耳其人越过幼发拉底河到1453年攻陷君士坦丁堡；这在当时很有影响，今天却很少有人这样读。原文四个词前面只有一个冠词，较自然的意思是神所定的一个时刻，而不是一段要加起来计算的时间。」
- en: "Historicists (e.g. E. B. Elliott in the nineteenth century) once added up 'the hour, the day, the month, and the year' by the day-for-a-year principle as about 396 years, reading it as the period from the Turks' crossing of the Euphrates in the eleventh century to the fall of Constantinople in 1453; this was influential in its day, but few read it so now. In Greek a single article governs all four words, which most naturally points to one appointed moment rather than a span to be added up."

### M5. 09.md §深读 9:20-21, point 1: the purpose of the plagues stated as settled
**Location:** 「可见启示录的灾难有一个目的：叫人悔改。」 / "So the plagues in Revelation have a purpose: to bring people to repentance."
**Problem:** This is the same disputed point as M3, stated here as fact. It needs to agree with the hedged wording in 08.
**Suggested fix:**
- zh: 「「仍旧不悔改」这句话表明，悔改本来是人面对这些灾难时应有的回应。许多释经者认为，这些灾难也有呼召人悔改的作用；毕尔等人则强调，它们像埃及的十灾一样，显明了人心的刚硬。到了第11章，在两个见证人的见证以后，确实有人「恐惧，归荣耀给天上的神」（11:13）。可是这里，人仍旧不悔改。」
- en: "'They did not repent' shows that repentance was the fitting response to these plagues. Many interpreters hold that the plagues also serve as a call to repentance; Beale and others stress that, like the plagues of Egypt, they expose the hardness of the human heart. In chapter 11, after the testimony of the two witnesses, some indeed 'were terrified and gave glory to the God of heaven' (11:13). But here, people do not repent."
