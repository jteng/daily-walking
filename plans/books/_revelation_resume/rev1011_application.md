## 10.md

### S1 (should-fix): the meditation rebuilds Revelation 02's tired-servant scene, and its fix is the 02 / Galatians 6 fix
**Location:** 默想 / Meditation, paragraphs 1 and 4, and the last two sentences.
- 「她心里已经定了：做到年底就退下来。也许神已经用完她了。」 / "She has already decided: she will stop at the end of the year. Perhaps God has finished with her."
- 「她也可以老实地告诉牧师：我很累，需要有人和我一同承担。如果她确实需要休息一段时间，那也不是被神开除；差遣她的那一位，也是喂养她的那一位。」 / "She might also tell her pastor honestly ... The One who sends her is also the One who feeds her."

**Problem:** Revelation 02 already has the worn-out Sunday-school / church servant whose Bible reading became lesson prep, who resents others, and whose fix is 「请长执分担，或者暂停一项服事，并不等于不爱主」. 10 repeats the same person, the same lesson-prep detail and the same relief valve. That relief valve is also Galatians 6 ("letting others carry part of the load"). The ending "not dismissed by God" echoes the retired "I am not superfluous" ending (1 Corinthians note). What only 10:9-11 has is the *word itself*: sweet in the mouth, rejected by the world, eaten again before the new sending. The boy's line 「那些都是讲给小孩子听的故事」 is the strongest thing here, and it should carry the meditation. Make the bitterness about the word being scorned, and about her own quiet doubt. The weariness can stay as background. Then let the oath of 10:6-7 answer the doubt and end on Christ. This also stops 10 ending on the seventh trumpet right before 11 ends on 11:15, which is the seventh trumpet too.

**Suggested rewrite (three linked edits):**

(a) Paragraph 1, replace the last two sentences:
- zh: 「她心里已经定了：做到年底就退下来。更叫她害怕的，是一个她不敢说出口的念头：也许那孩子说得对，那些真的只是讲给小孩子听的故事。」
- en: "She has already decided: she will stop at the end of the year. What frightens her more is a thought she dares not say aloud: perhaps the boy was right, and they really are just stories for little kids."

(b) Paragraph 4, replace everything from 「林姊妹也许可以在这个星期六」 / "This Saturday, Sister Lin might" to the end of the paragraph:
- zh: 「林姊妹也许可以在这个星期六，先不打开教师手册，像一个饿了的人那样，自己读一遍下周要讲的经文，不为备课，只为听神对她说话。当年叫她感动的，不是她讲得好，而是那些故事本身：挪亚的方舟，大卫在旷野的等候，都指向那一位要来的主。这话在她口中曾经是甜的，因为它是真的。差遣她的那一位，也是喂养她的那一位；年底是继续教，还是把班交给别人，可以等她吃饱了，再和教会一起安静地决定。」
- en: "This Saturday, Sister Lin might leave the teacher's manual closed at first and, like someone who is hungry, read next week's passage for herself—not to prepare a lesson, but to hear God speak to her. What moved her in those early years was not her teaching but the stories themselves: Noah's ark and David waiting in the wilderness both point to the Lord who was to come. The word was sweet in her mouth because it is true. The One who sends her is also the One who feeds her; whether she goes on teaching after the year ends or hands the class to someone else can be decided later, quietly and with her church, once she has eaten."

(c) Last paragraph, replace the final three sentences (from 「保罗说，神的奥秘」 / "Paul speaks of" to the end):
- zh: 「那孩子说，那些是讲给小孩子听的故事；直活到永远的神却指着自己所造的天地起誓，这些都是必要成全的佳音。保罗说，神的奥秘「就是基督」（西2:2）。林姊妹十二年来指给孩子们看的，就是这一位：他为她流了血，也仍在呼唤那个离开的孩子。」
- en: "The boy called them stories for little kids; the God who lives forever has sworn by the heaven and earth he made that they are good news, and they will be fulfilled. Paul speaks of \"God's mystery, which is Christ\" (Col 2:2). For twelve years Sister Lin has been pointing children to this Lord: he shed his blood for her, and he is still calling the boy who left."

(Col 2:2 is already in the draft and was cleared by the theology review. Nothing new is quoted. The ending now lands on Christ rather than on the oath. Hebrews 06 and 07 already end on oaths, and 11 ends on the seventh trumpet.)

### C1 (consider): let grace reach the boy she shouted at
**Location:** Meditation paragraph 3, after 「她可以把这些都带到他面前，不必先把自己整理好。」 / "She can bring all of it to him without first putting herself in order."
**Problem:** The confession is well grounded in 1 John 1:7, but the one person she wronged is left out. In Chinese church life adults seldom apologize to children. A small, concrete fruit of being washed would land, and it does not turn into a task list.
**Suggested addition:**
- zh: 「被洗净的人，也可以在下个主日蹲下来，对那个男孩说一声：老师那天不该吼你。」
- en: "And someone who has been washed can crouch down next Sunday and tell that boy, \"I shouldn't have shouted at you that day.\""

### C2 (consider): the sweetness of 10:9-10 for the reader who has never tasted it
**Location:** 深读 10:8-11 point 5, 「先领受神的话，让它在我们里面甜，也在我们里面苦」 / "let it be sweet in us and bitter in us".
**Problem:** This is accurate. Some readers, though, have never found Scripture sweet. If S1(b) is applied, the meditation gives the reason the word is sweet (it is true and it points to Christ), and this point could say so briefly. Optional; leave it if length is tight.
**Suggested addition (end of point 5):**
- zh: 「这话之所以甜，不是因为我们读的时候心里总有感觉，而是因为它所说的那一位是美好的。」
- en: "The word is sweet not because we always feel something when we read it, but because the One it speaks of is good."

## 11.md

### S1 (should-fix): the earlier signed reports are a safety matter, and 「在需要的地方去改正」 is too vague
**Location:** Meditation paragraph 3: 「小吴不必把自己的胆怯和妥协藏起来；他可以在这位被钉的主面前承认，在需要的地方去改正，因为主的血足以遮盖这一切。」 / "Xiao Wu need not hide his cowardice and compromises; he can confess them before the crucified Lord and put things right where that is needed, because the Lord's blood is enough to cover it all."
**Problem:** Xiao Wu has signed "one or two" inspection reports he knew were wrong, at an engineering firm. People may be living or working under those structures. "Put things right where that is needed" lets a reader confess privately and leave real danger in place. The order is also slightly off: it puts the repair before the ground of grace in the same clause. 13's version of this scene, which is being replaced, was the one that said this plainly. Now that 11 alone keeps the inspection scene, it has to carry it. Ground the repair in forgiveness, so that it does not read as earning forgiveness.
**Suggested rewrite:**
- zh: 「小吴不必把自己的胆怯和妥协藏起来；他可以在这位被钉的主面前承认，因为主的血足以遮盖这一切。正因为他不必再保住自己的记录，他也可以把从前签过的那一两份报告告诉该知道的人，因为那关乎别人的安全。这样做也许要付代价，但这不是去赚赦免，而是一个蒙了赦免的人，不再需要遮掩什么。」
- en: "Xiao Wu need not hide his cowardice and compromises; he can confess them before the crucified Lord, because the Lord's blood is enough to cover it all. And because he no longer has to protect his record, he can tell the people who need to know about the one or two reports he signed before, since other people's safety depends on them. It may cost him; but he is not earning forgiveness. He is a forgiven man who no longer needs to cover anything."
(Caller: make sure 13's new scenario does not bring the report back.)

### C1 (consider): the church of eight or nine broke itself, and Ezekiel 37 speaks to exactly that
**Location:** Meditation paragraph 4: 「那间只剩八九个人的教会也是一样。世人庆祝了「三天半」……」 / "So it is with the church of eight or nine..."
**Problem:** The church shrank through its own quarrel, yet paragraph 4 treats it simply as a mocked witness. That lets the church off more lightly than paragraph 2 lets Xiao Wu off. The "small church under pressure" image is also Revelation 05's, with its half-empty living room. The section body already notes the Ezekiel 37 echo in 11:11, and that is the gospel answer here: the bones were Israel dead under judgment for their own sin, saying "our hope is lost", and God breathed into them. This makes the corporate application more honest and keeps it apart from 05. It also drops the "pray for those who oppose me" task, which 2 Timothy 03 already used in an almost identical workplace scene (refusing to falsify a report, scorned by colleagues, separating self-caused suffering, praying for opponents).
**Suggested rewrite of paragraph 4:**
- zh: 「那间只剩八九个人的教会也是一样。以西结看见的那些骸骨，不是被别人害死的英雄，而是因自己的罪被掳的百姓；他们自己说：「我们的骨头枯干了，我们的指望失去了」（结37:11）。神的气息却进入了他们里面。这间教会的伤，有一些是外面的人给的，也有一些是自己吵出来的；它需要的不只是安慰，也需要彼此认罪、和好。可是叫它站起来的，仍旧是「从神那里」来的生气。世人庆祝了「三天半」，见证人却传道了「一千二百六十天」（11:3、9）。小吴不需要为了挽回面子去反击那些同事；他可以继续诚实地做他的工作，盼望那些举杯的人有一天也像那「其余的」人一样，「归荣耀给天上的神」（11:13）。」
- en: "So it is with the church of eight or nine. The bones Ezekiel saw were not heroes killed by others but a people exiled for their own sin, who said, \"Our bones are dried up, and our hope is lost\" (Ezek 37:11). Yet God's breath entered them. Some of this church's wounds came from outside, and some it quarreled into itself; it needs not only comfort but confession and reconciliation with one another. Yet what will raise it is still the breath of life \"from God.\" The world celebrated for \"three and a half days,\" while the witnesses prophesied for \"1,260 days\" (11:3, 9). Xiao Wu does not need to strike back at his colleagues to save face; he can go on doing his work honestly, hoping that one day those who raised their glasses will, like \"the rest,\" give \"glory to the God of heaven\" (11:13)."
(Ezek 37:11 checked with bible_lookup: CUV 「我们的骨头枯干了，我们的指望失去了」; ESV "Our bones are dried up, and our hope is lost". It still needs the publisher's quote check.)

### C2 (consider): keep the scale honest for readers under real persecution
**Location:** Meditation paragraph 1, last sentence: 「小吴有时觉得，自己和教会的见证，就像倒在大街上的尸首，旁边的人正在庆祝。」
**Problem:** Many Chinese readers know believers who have been detained, or have lost jobs or homes, for Christ. Comparing office teasing straight to the slain witnesses can sound light to them. One sentence acknowledges the gap and still honors the real sting.
**Suggested addition (after that sentence):**
- zh: 「他知道，这比不上那些为主坐监、丢了工作甚至丢了性命的弟兄姊妹；可是被人当成笑话的滋味，也是真的。」
- en: "He knows this is nothing beside brothers and sisters who have been imprisoned, or lost their jobs or even their lives, for Christ; but the taste of being made a joke is real too."

### C3 (consider, cross-chapter): closing-prayer shape
**Location:** Meditation, last paragraph (prayer, then 11:15).
**Problem:** 09 and 13 also end with a written prayer, and 09's ends prayer then verse, like 11's. 11's prayer is good: it is Christ-centered and confesses "spoiled by me". Keep it. Tell the 13 rewrite not to end in a prayer, so that three of 09-13 do not share that shape. Also, 10 should no longer end on the seventh trumpet (see 10 S1(c)), so that 11 alone carries 11:15.

## What works well (keep)
- **10:** The two-sided reading of the bitterness ("not failure" next to "mixed with other things") is pastorally wise. It neither shames the faithful sufferer nor excuses resentment. 1 John 1:7 arrives before any task, and 「不必先把自己整理好」 is grace stated plainly. 「神说的是『再』」 is the best single line in the chapter: the God who sends again is the gospel answer to the one who thinks she is finished. The "not a date" and "word to those who love to calculate" points serve the Eastern Lightning context without repeating 00.
- **11:** The second paragraph's honesty, with the witness "spoiled by himself", is exactly the reader who has failed. The pivot to Mark 15:31 places Xiao Wu's mockery at the cross, where Christ died for the mockers and for the silent disciples. 「见证人站起来，不是因为他们的记录完美」 is grace, not moralism. The 要点 lines throughout point to God's keeping, Spirit and word ("not a sword"), and the Luke 9:54-55 guard against violence is valuable. The 11:18 note that God forgets no obscure servant offers delight as well as information.
