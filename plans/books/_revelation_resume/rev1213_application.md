Its old deacon has a true page he dreads, confessed many times, and partly forced on him by others ("他没有记错：案卷是真实的"). 12 has the same shape and the same picture: a long-serving church worker, a true record of one past sin, others' pressure named, grace answering it. Change the image to one that belongs to 12 itself: the accuser who has "no longer any place" in heaven (12:8), with his seat in the court now empty. Do not use Zechariah 3's change of garments, because 19 is built on garments that are "granted".

### Consider

**C12-a. "第一次说出这件事" contradicts paragraph 1.** P1 says she "多年以前也告诉过一位年长的传道". P4 zh: 「第一次说出这件事」→「多年来第一次又对人说出这件事」. en: "and told her for the first time" → "and, for the first time in years, told someone". Showing the trusted sister keeping the confidence would also model the church line in P3. Add after 「不是你。」: 「这件事，她没有再对别人说。」 / after "is not you.": "She told no one else."

**C12-b. 12:13-17 ends on the dragon's attack with no word of comfort.** The 要点 for that section was cut for length. The last line is now 「龙打不到坐在宝座上的那一位，就去攻击属他的人。」 / "so he attacks those who belong to him." The eagle's wings and the earth helping the woman are the comfort in this section. If space allows, add one clause: 「……就去攻击属他的人；可是背着妇人飞到旷野的，是那位曾如鹰背着以色列的神。」 / "…so he attacks those who belong to him; but the one who carries the woman into the wilderness is the God who bore Israel on eagles' wings."

**C12-c. Delight.** The meditation is accurate and tender. Its note of joy is only implied, though 12:12 opens with "rejoice, O heavens". One clause in P2 after "还留在宝座前的，是那位像是被杀过的羔羊" would give the reader something to delight in: 「所以诸天可以快乐（12:12），素珍也可以。」 / "That is why the heavens may rejoice (12:12), and Suzhen may too." Optional.

### Safeguarding and grace check on the abortion scene (asked for)
- Grace comes first. She had already confessed with tears, and the answer is the Lamb's blood ("已经为这件事付了代价"), Rom 8:33-34 in the body and Mic 7:19. Nothing in it tells her to make amends, serve more or prove herself. The theology reviewer already moved the Lord's Supper element away, so it does not repeat the 1 Corinthians theme.
- The sin is named without crushing her. Grief for the child is legitimate, and the child is entrusted to God without speculation.
- The wrong done to her is named (abandoned, pressured, alone) without becoming an excuse.
- The church is warned against gossip. Disclosure is to one trusted person in private, not a public testimony. That is right, and nothing should push women to testify publicly.
- Missing until A12-2: a word to the man or family member who pressured her, and to a reader facing that pressure now. With A12-2 applied the care is complete.
- Retired frame: the accuser is the text of 12:10, so the subject is legitimate. The draft reports her sense of being unfit rather than quoting an inner voice, which keeps clear of the retired "quoted accusing inner voice" frame. Keep it that way. Do not add quoted accusations in revision.

### Works well (keep)
- 「这个控告者不一定要说谎；他常常拿真实的事来定人的罪」 and "the voice does not announce that the file is false": an honest, grace-centred hinge (keep it, with the A12-1 wording).
- 「被摔下去的是控告者，被投进深海的是罪；还留在宝座前的，是那位像是被杀过的羔羊」: Christ at the centre, and adoration.
- The friend's 「被摔下去的，不是你」, and the hospital visit to the premature baby. The return to her own ministry is quiet and concrete, not a triumph.
- The 要点 lines (12:1-6; 12:10-12) point away from the reader to the throne and the Lamb.
- The ending on 12:10, as the brief asks. It differs from 11 (11:15), 14 and 20.

### Should-fix

**A13-1. The 财神 opening-rite scene repeats 02, and the 666/chip expert repeats 00.**
- Location: 默想 paragraphs 1, 2 and 4.
- Problem:
  - (a) 02.md 深读 2:14 already gives exactly this as its present-day Pergamum compromise: 「今天也许是公司开业时拜财神，或生意场上推不掉的应酬」. 09.md's body also lists 财神 among today's idols. 13's meditation would be the third use and the only full scene, and Pergamum is named again in its paragraph 2.

The pressure is the 铁饭碗: a job form whose 宗教信仰 line she is told to fill with 「无」. This is the forehead (what I confess) and the hand (what I sign), tied to a livelihood, and it echoes 2:13 「坚守我的名」. No other meditation uses it. 11 has one passing detail where 小吴 says he 「不算是」 a believer. Here that becomes a whole scene with a different stake, and the wording below avoids 「含糊」 so it does not echo 11. Keep 小杜 (unique). Making 小杜 a young woman varies the cast after 11's 小吴.
- Caller's judgment: this scene touches state hiring forms. It is realistic for mainland readers and fits Revelation 13 and its Romans 13 / Acts 5:29 body. The story describes her choice and does not lay down a rule, and the small group explicitly does not decide for her. If the team judges it too exposed for mainland readers, a fallback that avoids the state is a private-sector sales job: winning the region's key client depends on joining the client's annual temple-blessing trip and buying and offering 开光 incense with them. That is worship tied to trade, but it stays close to 02, so I prefer the form scene.
