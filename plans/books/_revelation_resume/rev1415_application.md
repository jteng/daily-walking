## 14.md

### S1 (should-fix): the surname clashes with 09's 老郑
- **Location:** 默想 / Meditation, every mention.
- **Problem:** 郑 is already used for 09's 老郑. Surnames already used across the book: 陈, 刘, 李, 郑, 黄, 周, 王 (22's 王奶奶), 林 (10's 林姊妹), 秀兰. 赵 is not used in any Revelation chapter.
- **Rewrite:** replace all four ::zh mentions 「郑妈妈」 with 「赵妈妈」 (「郑妈妈八十六岁过世」, 「郑妈妈是谁？」, 「郑妈妈若在这里」, 「郑妈妈四十年的劳苦」). Replace all four ::en mentions "Mother Zheng" with "Mother Zhao".

### C1 (consider): 执事 clashes with the story it is in, and repeats 20
- **Location:** 默想, 「会友名单上，她的名字后面只写着执事两个字。」 / "in the membership list the only word after her name was 'deaconess.'"
- **Problem:** In many Chinese churches 执事 is a recognised office, so the line works against the point that nobody noticed her. 20's meditation also has a long-serving 执事.
- **Rewrite:** zh 「会友名单上，她的名字后面什么职分也没有写。」 / en "in the membership list there was no title after her name at all."

### C2 (consider): 7:14 robes again; use this chapter's own "bought"
- **Location:** 默想 para 2, 「我若在主里面，羔羊的血已经把我的衣裳洗白了（7:14）。」 / "If I am in the Lord, the Lamb's blood has already washed my robe white (7:14)."
- **Problem:** 7:14 is already used in the meditations of 00 and 19, and twice in 14's own body. 14:3-4 makes the same point and belongs to this chapter.
- **Rewrite:** zh 「我若在主里面，我就是羔羊用自己的血从地上买来的人（14:3-4；5:9），这价他已经付清了。」 / en "If I am in the Lord, I am one of those the Lamb bought from the earth with his own blood (14:3–4; 5:9), and he has paid the price in full."

### C3 (consider): "back row" repeats 06
- **Location:** 默想 para 1, 「坐在后排的我，心里冒出一个念头」 / "Sitting in the back row, I found a thought rising".
- **Problem:** 06's meditation is built on hiding in the back row (「也不用躲在教会的后排」), and 08's 李阿姨 sits 「在角落里」. Small, but easy to change.
- **Rewrite:** zh 「我站在门边，心里冒出一个念头」 / en "Standing by the door, I found a thought rising".

### C4 (consider): endurance shown only as weighing costs (14:12)
- **Location:** 深读 14:12-13 §1, 「有了第三位天使的警告，就看得出圣徒为什么要忍耐：宁可在地上受兽的逼迫，也不要受神永远的审判。」 / "...they would rather suffer the beast's persecution on earth than God's eternal judgment."
- **Problem:** As written, the only reason for enduring is fear of a worse outcome. The chapter opens with belonging to the Lamb (14:1, 4), and endurance should flow from that.
- **Rewrite:** zh 「……宁可在地上受兽的逼迫，也不要受神永远的审判；更深的原因是，他们额上写着羔羊的名（14:1），宁可失去一切，也不肯离开那位为他们被杀的主。」 / en "...rather than God's eternal judgment; and, more deeply, because the Lamb's name is on their foreheads (14:1), they would rather lose everything than leave the one who was slain for them."

### Note for the coordinator (no change to 14)
14 ends on John 19:30 「成了」. 21's meditation also grounds itself in 「都成了」 (21:6) tied to the cross, and 16 has 「成了」 as a body heading. Keep 14's ending, which is the strongest use, and ask 21's application review to make sure its resolution does not lean on "it is finished" too.

### What works in 14 (keep)
- The meditation is grace-shaped: the dying criminal (Luke 23:42-43) shows that the blessing rests on being "in the Lord", not on forty years of service. Only after that does it say that deeds done in him are not lost (Heb 6:10). The order is right: gospel first, then the call.
- Honest about mixed motives (serving 「为了面子」, 「一边做一边抱怨」), and those motives are named as forgiven, not left as guilt.
- 「他的工作走在我们前面，我们的才能跟在后面」 and the ending, where the Spirit's 「是的」 is spoken in the Lord who said 「成了」, keep the reader's eyes on Christ.
- The 14:6-11 closing paragraph refuses both scare-preaching and silence about judgment and points to 1 Thess 1:10 and Rom 8:1. Keep it.
- 人过留名，雁过留声 fits Chinese readers naturally.

## 15.md

### S1 (should-fix): every hard word to God called prayer, with no line about where words cross over
- **Location:** 默想 para 2, 「对神说重话，向他倒出心里的苦，也是向他祷告；约伯和诗篇的作者都这样做过；神回答了约伯，也没有丢弃他们。」 / "Saying hard things to God, pouring out bitterness before him, is also prayer; Job and the psalmists did it; God answered Job, and he did not cast them off."
- **Problem:** Lament is prayer, but the sentence implies that every bitter word is automatically prayer. That leaves out Job's own repentance (Job 42:6), and it leaves out the forgiveness that 黄弟兄's guilt (「事后又不敢承认」) actually needs. Grace should meet the guilt, not only approve the lament. Also, 21's meditation has 秀兰 who 「对神说过很多难听的话」, so the theme appears in two chapters (see C3).
- **Rewrite:** zh 「把心里的苦和疑问倒在神面前，本身就是祷告；诗篇里有许多这样的哀歌，约伯也这样向神呼求过。有些话也许说过了头，约伯后来也在神面前说『我厌恶自己，在尘土和炉灰中懊悔』（伯42:6）；可是神回答了他，没有丢弃他。黄弟兄那些不敢承认的重话，也可以原原本本地带到神面前；羔羊的血连这些也遮盖了。」 / en "Pouring out bitterness and questions before God is itself prayer; the Psalms are full of such laments, and Job cried out to God in the same way. Some words may go too far, and Job at last said, 'I despise myself, and repent in dust and ashes' (Job 42:6); yet God answered him and did not cast him off. The hard words Brother Huang has not dared to admit can be brought to God just as they are; the Lamb's blood covers these too."

### S2 (should-fix): the dead daughter is never mentioned again
- **Location:** 默想 para 3, 「有一天，他也要站在玻璃海边，手里拿着神所赐的琴。」 / "One day he too will stand beside the sea of glass with a harp that God has given."
- **Problem:** A grieving father hearing "he too will stand there" will at once ask "and my daughter?" Saying nothing about her will feel cold to a bereaved parent. One sentence can hold her in Christ's hands without claiming more than Scripture says (07 treats the dead the same way).
- **Rewrite:** insert before that sentence: zh 「他的女儿，也在那位曾抱起小孩子、给他们按手、为他们祝福的主手中（可10:16）。」 / en "His daughter, too, is in the hands of the Lord who took the children in his arms, laid his hands on them and blessed them (Mark 10:16)."

### C1 (consider): a scholarly aside in the middle of the meditation
- **Location:** 默想 para 3, 「按后来犹太文献（《米示拿》）所记的逾越节习惯，饭后唱的是哈利路诗篇的后半部分（约是诗115-118篇）；马可没有说明是哪一首，但这很可能就是当晚所唱的。」 / "By the Passover custom recorded later in Jewish sources (the Mishnah), the closing part of the Hallel (roughly Psalms 115–118) was sung after the meal; Mark does not say what they sang, but this is very likely it."
- **Problem:** Correct, but at the most tender moment it reads like a footnote and slows the turn to Christ.
- **Rewrite:** zh 「按后来犹太文献所记的逾越节习惯，饭后要唱诗篇第115到118篇；马可没有说明，但当晚很可能唱的就是这几篇。」 / en "By the Passover custom recorded in later Jewish sources, Psalms 115–118 were sung after the meal; Mark does not say, but those are very likely what they sang."

### C2 (consider, variety; act in 16 rather than 15): the same night of the Passion in two chapters in a row
- **Location:** 15 默想 para 3 (Mark 14:26, the hymn on the night of betrayal, going into the deep waters of judgment) and 16 默想 (Gethsemane, Mark 14:36 and John 18:11, drinking the cup).
- **Problem:** Back-to-back meditations both resolve on "that night he went into God's judgment for us". The images differ (song and cup), but a reader of 15 and then 16 will feel the same turn twice.
- **Suggestion:** keep 15's Mark 14:26. In 16 the cup is the right anchor, but its application review should avoid 「那一夜」 / "that night" framing and should not name the Mount of Olives walk again.

### C3 (consider, variety; note for 14, 15 and 21)
- Two meditations in a row open on a believer's death (14 the funeral, 15 the child's death; 07 is also a death). The angles differ (worth of service vs grief and worship), so no change is required in 14 or 15, but no later chapter should open on a death.
- "Hard words said to God" appears in 15 (黄弟兄) and 21 (秀兰, 「对神说过很多难听的话」, 「对神说过难听话的人可以来」). After S1, 15 handles it fully; ask 21's reviser to drop or reframe 秀兰's line, for example as anger at her mother only.

### What works in 15 (keep)
- 「神没有要求还在海里的人，用海边的声音来唱。」 This is the best line in the pair and lands with the suffering reader exactly. Keep it as is.
- The "overcome with praise" and "sit out Easter" responses are named as unhelpful without mocking those who said them. Letting the church sing for him while he listens is a concrete, gentle picture of bearing one another's burdens.
- Psalm 137:4 shows that the question itself was taken into Israel's songbook, which is the right Scripture for this reader.
- The ending is God-centred, not self-centred: the conquerors sing neither their endurance nor their losses, and the reader is invited to say 15:4 "however quietly". This is adoration without a task, as WORKFLOW asks for variety.
- Body: the 15:2 note that 「得胜」 is not career, health or church growth is a needed corrective to prosperity readings. The 15:3-4 point (Bauckham: the song never mentions the singers) prepares the meditation well.
