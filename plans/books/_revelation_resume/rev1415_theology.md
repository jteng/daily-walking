## MUST-FIX

### M1 (15.md, Meditation, para 3): "the song of the Lamb" explained as the Hallel Jesus sang; the Hallel is pinned too precisely
- ZH: 「它叫「羔羊的歌」，因为是羔羊先唱的。」……「按犹太人的习惯，那很可能是诗篇第113-118篇。」
- EN: "It is called 'the song of the Lamb,' because the Lamb sang first." … "by Jewish custom it was very likely Psalms 113–118."
- **Problem:** This gives a homiletical link as the reason for the title in 15:3. That contradicts the chapter's own exegesis in 15:3-4 point 1, where the song is "of the Lamb" because it celebrates the greater exodus the Lamb accomplished. On the Hallel: our evidence for Passover practice is the Mishnah (Pesahim 10.5-7, c. AD 200). It has Psalm 113 (or 113-114) sung before the meal and the rest of the Hallel (114/115-118) after it. So the hymn at Mark 14:26 is, at most, probably the closing part of the Hallel. Mark does not say.
- **Fix ZH:** 「它叫「羔羊的歌」，因为它唱的是羔羊所成就的拯救。而且羔羊自己也曾在最黑的夜里唱过歌：主耶稣被卖的那一夜，在逾越节的筵席上，他和门徒「唱了诗，就出来，往橄榄山去」（可14:26）。按后来犹太文献（《米示拿》）所记的逾越节习惯，饭后唱的是「哈利路」诗篇的后半部分（约是诗115-118篇）；马可没有说明是哪一首，但这很可能就是当晚所唱的。」
- **Fix EN:** "It is called 'the song of the Lamb' because it sings of the salvation the Lamb accomplished. And the Lamb himself once sang on the darkest night: on the night he was betrayed, at the Passover meal, the Lord Jesus and his disciples 'had sung a hymn' and 'went out to the Mount of Olives' (Mark 14:26). By the Passover custom recorded later in Jewish sources (the Mishnah), the closing part of the Hallel (roughly Psalms 115–118) was sung after the meal; Mark does not say what they sang, but this is very likely it."
- Keep the rest of the paragraph ("He did not sing it from the far shore…").

### M2 (15.md, 15:5-8 point 2): the 15:6 variant is described the wrong way round
- ZH: 「有些抄本作石头，不作细麻……多数译本采用「细麻衣」。」 EN: "some manuscripts read 'stone' instead of 'linen'… Most translations read 'linen.'"
- **Problem:** "Stone" (*lithon*) is the reading of the strongest witnesses (Alexandrinus, Ephraemi). It is also the reading printed in the critical text (NA28/UBS5) as the harder reading. The ESV footnote gives "stone" as a manuscript alternative. Most translations print "linen" anyway, because "clothed in stone" is so hard. As written, the draft implies "stone" is the weakly attested reading.
- **Fix ZH:** 「**抄本的差异：** 这里有两个只差一个字母的希腊词：细麻（*linon*）和石头（*lithon*）。几部重要的古抄本作石头，现代通行的希腊文校勘本也采用这个较难的读法，有人联想到以西结书28:13的宝石；可是「穿着石头」很难理解，所以和合本和多数译本都译作「细麻衣」。」
- **Fix EN:** "**A manuscript difference:** two Greek words here differ by one letter, linen (*linon*) and stone (*lithon*). Several important early manuscripts read 'stone,' and the standard critical Greek text adopts it as the harder reading; some link it with the precious stones of Ezekiel 28:13. But 'clothed in stone' is so difficult that the CUV and most translations read 'linen.'"

### M3 (14.md, 14:6-11 point 4, first bullet): 20:10 cited for the fate of human unbelievers
- ZH: 「这里的「昼夜不得安宁」和20:10的「必昼夜受痛苦，直到永永远远」，都说明不信的人要永远有知觉地承受刑罚。」 EN: "…'no rest, day or night' here and 'tormented day and night forever and ever' in 20:10 show that unbelievers will consciously bear punishment forever."
- **Problem:** The subjects in 20:10 are the devil, the beast and the false prophet, not human unbelievers. The traditional argument runs: 14:11 is said of human worshipers, and 20:15 and 21:8 cast unbelievers into that same lake. Annihilationists press exactly this point, so the draft should state the argument accurately.
- **Fix ZH:** 「……认为，14:11的「昼夜不得安宁」说的是拜兽的人；20:10说魔鬼、兽和假先知在火湖里「必昼夜受痛苦，直到永永远远」，而20:15说名字没有记在生命册上的人也被扔在这同一个火湖里。所以他们认为，不信的人要永远有知觉地承受刑罚。」
- **Fix EN:** "…holds that 'no rest, day or night' (14:11) is said of human worshipers of the beast, and that 20:10 says the devil, beast and false prophet 'will be tormented day and night forever and ever' in the lake into which, by 20:15, everyone not found in the book of life is also thrown; so unbelievers will consciously bear punishment forever."

### S1 (14.md, 14:6-11 point 1, last sentence): Seventh-day Adventist paragraph (fairness and placement)
- ZH: 「基督复临安息日会把14:6-12称为三天使的信息……其他福音派教会一般认为……」
- **Problem:** The summary of the Adventist position is accurate (Fundamental Belief 13, the remnant and its mission; the link from 14:7 to the Sabbath). Two things need changing:
  - **Fairness to Adventists:** the echo of Exod 20:11 in 14:7 is real, and non-Adventist commentators note it too. Saying so is fairer, and it makes the actual point of difference clear: the inference from that echo to a Saturday obligation.
  - **Labels:** "other evangelical churches" quietly settles whether Adventists count as evangelical, which is itself disputed.
  - **Placement:** the paragraph is tacked onto the sentence "these readings are not wholly opposed," which belongs to the previous list.
- **Fix ZH (new short paragraph):** 「基督复临安息日会把14:6-12称为「三天使的信息」，认为这是神交给他们这个运动的末世使命。他们指出，14:7「创造天地海和众水泉源的」呼应第四条诫命（出20:11），因此把这节与守星期六为安息日连在一起。许多释经者也看见这里有创造和第四诫的回音，但大多数新教教会认为，这里的重点是呼召万民敬拜创造主，不是规定守哪一天（参西2:16-17）；这三位天使的宣告是对历世历代众教会说的。」
- **Fix EN:** "The Seventh-day Adventist Church calls 14:6–12 'the three angels' messages' and holds that they are the end-time commission given to its movement. It points out that 'him who made heaven and earth, the sea and the springs of water' (14:7) echoes the fourth commandment (Exod 20:11), and so links the verse with keeping Saturday as the Sabbath. Many interpreters also hear the echo of creation and the fourth commandment, but most Protestant churches take the point to be a call to the nations to worship the Creator, not a ruling on which day to keep (cf. Col 2:16–17), and take the three proclamations as addressed to the churches in every age."

### S3 (14.md, 14:6-11 "要点 / The point"): the gospel ground is left implicit

### S6 (15.md, 15:1 point 3): seals/trumpets/bowls, trim to a pointer to 06 plus the bowl-specific material
- ZH: 「本研读在第00章介绍过两种主要的读法……」 EN: "This study introduced the two main readings in chapter 00…"
- **Problems:**

  - Repeating Victorinus, Hendriksen, Beale, Walvoord, Ladd and Osborne duplicates 06.
  - "两种读法都同意" / "Both readings agree" follows what are effectively three positions.
  - Keep the bowl-specific material: the trumpet/bowl parallel in order (earth, sea, rivers, sun, darkness, Euphrates, final upheaval; 8:7-11:19; 16:2-21), and the seventh trumpet "containing" the bowls in the telescoping view.
- **Fix ZH (whole point):** 「**3. 七碗和印、号的关系。** 导读和第6章已经介绍过重述、先后套叠和介乎两者之间的读法，这里只补充七碗特有的一点：七号和七碗的次序非常相似，都临到地、海、江河、日头，都有黑暗、伯拉大河和末了的大震动（8:7-11:19；16:2-21）。主张重述的人认为，这说明七碗是更强烈地重讲同一段时期；主张先后发生的人认为，第七号「包含」了七碗，是主再来以前最后的一连串审判。无论哪一种读法，七碗都是最后、最完全的审判，好像出埃及的十灾（第16章会再看到这些对照），而且是从宝座和天上的殿发出来的。」

The bowls, the seals and the trumpets.** The introduction and chapter 6 set out the recapitulation, sequential-telescoping and mixed readings; here we add only what is particular to the bowls. The trumpets and bowls follow a strikingly similar order: both strike the earth, the sea, the rivers and the sun, and both include darkness, the Euphrates and a final great upheaval (8:7–11:19; 16:2–21). ### S7 (15.md, 15:5-8 point 3): the 5:8 / 8:3-4 link is overstated
- ZH: 「他们的祷告盛在金碗里，升到神面前（8:3-4）；现在从同样的金碗里，倒出神公义的审判。」 EN similar.
- **Problems:**
  - 8:3-5 has a golden *censer* (*libanōtos*), not bowls (*phialē*).
  - "The same golden bowls" is an interpretive link, made by Beale among others, not something the text states.
  - The draft misses a stronger textual tie: in 5:8 it is the four living creatures (with the elders) who hold the bowls of prayer, and in 15:7 it is one of the four living creatures who hands out the bowls of wrath. And 8:5 shows prayer turning into judgment: the same censer is filled with fire from the altar and thrown on the earth.
- **Fix ZH:** 「……在5:8，拿着盛满众圣徒祈祷之金碗的，正是四活物和二十四位长老；在这里，把盛满神大怒之金碗交给天使的，也是四活物中的一个。8:3-5也有类似的次序：众圣徒的祈祷和香一同升到神面前，随后天使把同一个香炉盛满坛上的火，倒在地上。许多释经者（如毕尔）因此认为，七碗的审判是神对他百姓祷告的回应。神没有忘记他百姓的祷告。」
- **Fix EN:** "…In 5:8 it is the four living creatures and the elders who hold the golden bowls of the saints' prayers; here it is one of the four living creatures who gives the golden bowls of wrath. 8:3–5 has a similar sequence: the prayers of the saints rise with the incense before God, and then the angel fills the same censer with fire from the altar and throws it on the earth. Many interpreters (e.g. Beale) therefore see the bowl judgments as God's answer to his people's prayers. God has not forgotten them."

### S8 (15.md, 15:3-4 point 2): the clearest Exodus 15 echo is missing

- Then soften the Bauckham sentence so it does not contradict this: ZH 「出埃及记第15章大部分在唱「将马和骑马的投在海中」……」 / EN "Exodus 15 sings at length of 'the horse and his rider… thrown into the sea'…".

## CONSIDER

### C1 (14.md, 14:1-5 point 1): Zion, attribution and Old Testament background
- I am not confident that Bauckham takes 14:1 as specifically the *heavenly* Zion. Beale does.
- Suggest 「许多释经者（如毕尔）」 / "Many interpreters (e.g. Beale)".
- Add Joel 2:32 as the key Old Testament background, the delivered remnant on Mount Zion who call on the LORD's name. ZH: 「（参珥2:32「在锡安山……必有逃脱的人」）」. EN: "(cf. Joel 2:32, 'in Mount Zion… there shall be those who escape')".

### C2 (14.md, 14:13 point 3): parity, "Yes" vs ESV "Blessed indeed"
- The EN quotes the ESV "Blessed indeed" and then says the Spirit "answers, 'Yes.'"
- Add: "(Greek *nai*, 'yes'; the ESV renders it 'Blessed indeed')".

### C3 (14.md, 14:20 point 4): 1,600 stadia
- Optionally add, in one clause, the older observation that 1,600 stadia is roughly the length of the land of Israel, which some take to mean the whole land. ZH: 「也有人指出，一千六百斯塔迪大约是巴勒斯坦南北的长度。」
- The symbolic 4×4×100 reading can stay as the main one.

### C4 (14.md, 14:14-20 "要点"): Heb 13:12
- The body hedges well ("the text does not draw that link").
- The point line then builds on it. That is acceptable, because the identity of the treader rests on 19:15 and Christ's suffering outside the gate is plain gospel.
- To match the hedge, a softer wording would be: 「……那位将来踹酒榨的，正是曾在城门外为我们受苦的那一位（来13:12）。」 The Mark 13:32 cross-reference at 14:15 is fine.

### C5 (15.md, 15:3-4 point 5): *hosios*
- "Devout" is a human sense of the word.
- Applied to God, *hosios* renders the idea of covenant holiness and faithfulness. Note LXX Deut 32:4, 「δίκαιος καὶ ὅσιος κύριος」, which ties this line to the song of Moses (point 1) and to 16:5.
- ZH: 「这里的「圣」（*hosios*）用在神身上，指他完全圣洁、守约可靠；七十士译本的申命记32:4（摩西之歌）正用这个词形容神。」
- EN: "*hosios*, used of God, means his utter holiness and covenant faithfulness; the Greek Old Testament uses it of God in Deuteronomy 32:4, the song of Moses."

### C6 (15.md, Meditation para 2): Job
- 「约伯和诗篇的作者都这样做过，神没有因此丢弃他们」 is true (Job 42:7). But Job was also answered and humbled (38:1-42:6).
- A clause would keep the lament permission biblical without making it unqualified: 「神回答了约伯，也没有丢弃他」 / "God answered Job and did not cast him off".

### C7 (15.md, Meditation para 3): parity
- ZH 「所以这首歌今天可以由弟兄姊妹替陈弟兄唱」; EN "So this Easter the brothers and sisters can sing…". Align the two (今天 / today, or 今年复活节 / this Easter).

### C8 (15.md, Meditation para 3): gospel plainness
- 「为我们走过那片海」 is evocative, but it is the only atonement statement in the meditation.
- One plain clause would help: ZH 「他替我们担当了神的审判，死了，又复活了」; EN "he bore God's judgment in our place, died and rose again".
