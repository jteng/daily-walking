## SHOULD-FIX

### S1. 16.md, 默想 / Meditation, paras 1 and 5: the phone image is a retired frame
- Quoted: 「然后关掉了手机上的圣经」 / "and closes the Bible app on her phone"; 「这对关上手机的小雅」 / "That is good news for Xiaoya, who closed her phone".
- Problem: WORKFLOW retires "the phone as an image" (Romans 12, Ephesians 5). Here it is more than a detail: para 5 names her by it. A closed Bible makes the same point and pairs with the ending (「重新打开第16章」).
- Rewrite:
  - para 1: 「……然后把圣经合上了。」 / "...and closes her Bible."
  - para 5: 「这对合上圣经的小雅，对多年把审判轻轻带过的牧师，都是好消息。」 / "That is good news for Xiaoya, who closed her Bible, and for the pastor who for years passed lightly over judgment."

### S2. 17.md, 默想 / Meditation, para 4 ("two ledgers"): the pivot repeats 13 and 20
- Quoted: 「在同一章里，有两本账……方姊妹的名字写在那本册子上，比她做那个买房的梦早得多，也不因为平台倒闭而被涂抹；那是「被杀之羔羊生命册」（13:8），是用羔羊的血写上去的。」 / "In this chapter there are two ledgers... It is 'the book of life of the Lamb that was slain' (13:8), and her name is written there by the Lamb's blood."
- Problem: 13's meditation already resolves on 13:8 (「名字记在『被杀之羔羊生命册上』的人，不是靠自己的刚强站立的」), and 20's whole meditation is built on the same contrast: the books of deeds against "another book", ending "every name in that book was written by grace". Putting "Babylon's ledger vs the book of life" in 17 makes it the third use, and the one closest to 20. It also tells the reader as settled fact that her name "is not erased", a point 03 deliberately leaves as a Reformed/Arminian debate (3:5). 17:4 offers its own fresher way into the gospel, and it fits her idol (face, standing) exactly.
- Rewrite, option A (preferred; about the same length): replace the whole paragraph with:
  - zh: 「巴比伦穿着紫色和朱红色的衣服，叫人希奇。福音书里还有一件紫色的袍子。主耶稣被带出去钉十字架以前，兵丁「给他穿上紫袍，又用荆棘编做冠冕给他戴上，就庆贺他说：『恭喜，犹太人的王啊！』」（可15:17-18；参太27:28）。巴比伦的紫色是荣华，是人人羡慕的体面；他身上的紫袍却是戏弄，是当着众人的羞辱。方姊妹最怕的，就是在人面前抬不起头；这位真正的王，却甘心在众人面前被嘲笑、被吐唾沫，担当了罪人的羞辱，连她为了体面所犯的罪也在其中。所以她可以带着羞愧回到主面前，也回到教会里，因为她站立的根基从来不是她的眼光，而是这位穿过紫袍、为她流血的羔羊。」
  - en: "Babylon dazzles in purple and scarlet. The Gospels tell of another purple robe. Before Jesus was led out to be crucified, the soldiers \"clothed him in a purple cloak, and twisting together a crown of thorns, they put it on him. And they began to salute him, 'Hail, King of the Jews!'\" (Mark 15:17–18; see Matt 27:28). Babylon's purple is splendor, the standing everyone envies; the purple on him was mockery, shame in front of everyone. What Sister Fang fears most is being unable to hold up her head before others. This true King chose to be laughed at and spat on in public, bearing the shame of sinners, including the sin she committed for the sake of standing. So she can come back to the Lord, and back to the church, carrying her shame, because the ground she stands on was never her own judgment but the Lamb who wore that robe and shed his blood for her."
  - Quotations checked with bible_lookup.py (CUV 编做, not 编作). Mark 15:17-18 and Matt 27:28 are not used in any other meditation in the repo. Note: 14-16 and 18 already pivot on scenes from the Passion (the thief, the Last Supper hymn, Gethsemane, Judas's thirty pieces). The robe is a different image, but if the editor wants fewer Passion scenes in a row, use option B.
- Rewrite, option B (shorter; no new scene): delete para 4 and give the last paragraph this form:
  - zh: 「巴比伦的金杯会在「一时之间」成为荒场（18:19），那套房子也许永远买不成。可是这一章的结局不是巴比伦，而是羔羊：「他们与羔羊争战，羔羊必胜过他们，因为羔羊是万主之主、万王之王。同着羔羊的，就是蒙召、被选、有忠心的。」（17:14）这位羔羊得胜，不是靠金子和体面，而是靠被杀（5:9）；他的血也是为方姊妹流的。她在羔羊身边的位置，是从他的呼召开始的，不是从她的成功开始的；她所缺少的忠心，他也能在她身上造就。所以她可以带着羞愧回到主面前，也回到教会里。在亲戚面前，她也许永远抬不起头；可是她不是同着巴比伦，而是同着羔羊。得胜的，是羔羊，和同着他的人。」
  - en: "Babylon's golden cup will be laid waste \"in a single hour\" (18:19), and the flat may never be bought. But this chapter does not end with Babylon. It ends with the Lamb: \"They will make war on the Lamb, and the Lamb will conquer them, for he is Lord of lords and King of kings, and those with him are called and chosen and faithful.\" (17:14) This Lamb conquers not by gold or standing but by being slain (5:9), and his blood was shed for Sister Fang too. Her place beside the Lamb began with his call, not with her success, and the faithfulness she lacked he can build in her. So she can come back to the Lord, and back to the church, carrying her shame. Before her relatives she may never hold her head up; but she is not with Babylon. She is with the Lamb. The one who conquers is the Lamb, and those who are with him."

## CONSIDER

### C1. 16.md, 默想 para 1: name the motive behind the laugh
- Quoted: 「小雅也跟着笑，说这是古老的写法，现在不太这样理解，然后关掉了手机上的圣经。」
- Problem: the scene shows behavior but not the heart. Para 5 later speaks of "our cowardice and our silence", so the fear of man needs to be visible earlier. Without it, the reader may take her laugh as a doctrinal doubt rather than a wish to be accepted.
- Rewrite (add after the S1 change): 「她不是不信，只是不想在室友眼里显得古板、狭隘。」 / "It is not that she disbelieves it; she just does not want to look narrow and old-fashioned to her roommate."

### C2. 16.md, 默想 para 3: the sin that deserves the cup is too thin
- Quoted: 「我也曾在难处中埋怨神，而不是将荣耀归给他（16:9）……那「盛自己烈怒的酒杯」（16:19），我也配喝。」
- Problem: "complaining in hard times" alone can make the cup look out of proportion, and the doubter will not be convinced. The move "I am not only on the victims' side" is right. It needs the heart behind 16:9 (refusing to bow) and the harm I have done to others. Separately, 「配喝」 can read as "worthy to drink" (an honor; the body glosses *axios* as 配得). 「本来也该喝」 is clearer.
- Rewrite:
  - zh: 「……而我并不只站在受害者那一边。我也伤过人；我心里也有16:9那种宁可埋怨神、也不肯低头将荣耀归给他的硬。神的审判叫我难为情，是因为它也照出了我自己。那「盛自己烈怒的酒杯」（16:19），我本来也该喝。」
  - en: "...and I do not stand only on the side of the victims. I have wronged others too, and I know in myself the hardness of 16:9: the heart that would sooner complain against God than bow and give him glory. God's judgment embarrasses me because it shows me myself. The 'cup of the wine of the fury of his wrath' (16:19) is one I deserve to drink."

### C3. 16.md, 默想 para 5: let Xiaoya's witness to her roommate include the substitute
- Quoted: 「小雅也可以告诉室友：神向罪恶发怒，我为此感谢他；那碗本来也该倒在我身上。」
- Problem: to an unbelieving roommate, "God is angry and I thank him" plus "I deserved it" can sound grim or self-righteous unless it also points to Christ. A short line keeps the ending's full disclosure for the last paragraph.
- Rewrite: 「……那碗本来也该倒在我身上，可是有一位替我承担了。」 / "...that bowl should have been poured on me too, but Someone bore it in my place."

### C4. 16.md, 默想 last paragraph: Peter's sword is told twice
- Quoted: 「彼得拔刀，想替主挡住那一夜；主却叫他收刀，自己转身去接那杯（约18:11）。」
- Problem: para 4 already tells the sword and quotes John 18:11, and the final sentence quotes it a third time. Cutting the repeated scene sharpens the ending and saves length (16 is 35.7 KB).
- Rewrite of the opening of the last paragraph:
  - zh: 「七碗倒出来的时候，在基督里的人不在碗下，不是因为那杯被倒掉了，而是因为它已经被喝干了。那一夜，他本可以求父差遣十二营多天使来（太26:53），却没有求。所以今晚小雅可以重新打开第16章……」 (rest unchanged)
  - en: "When the seven bowls are poured out, those who are in Christ are not under them. That is not because the cup was poured away, but because it was drunk dry. That night he could have asked the Father for more than twelve legions of angels (Matt 26:53), and he did not ask. So tonight Xiaoya can open chapter 16 again..." (rest unchanged)

### C5. 16.md, variety note (no change required)
- 14 ends on the Spirit's 「是的」 (14:13), and 16 ends on Xiaoya's 「是的」 (16:7). 15 used the night of the betrayal (Mark 14:26), and 16 uses Gethsemane. Both are anchored in the text and the brief, and theology M1 already moved 16 off the sea of glass, so keep them. Later chapters (18-22) should not end on another "Yes/Amen" response or open a new scene from Passion night. 18 already uses Judas's price, and 21 the darkness at the cross.

### C6. 17.md, 默想 para 2: give the reader a question about their own golden cup
- Quoted: 「受吸引的不只是贪心的人，忠心的人也会被这样的光彩迷住。」
- Problem: the paragraph diagnoses well but stays with 方姊妹 and "people". One specific question turns it to the reader's own idol without guilt.
- Rewrite (insert after that sentence):
  - zh: 「我的金杯是什么？也许是一套房、一个头衔，也许是孩子在亲戚面前的体面。这些本身不一定是罪；可是当它们答应给我神没有应许的保障和尊荣时，我就已经开始希奇了。」
  - en: "What is my golden cup? Perhaps a flat, a title, or my child's standing before the relatives. These need not be sinful in themselves; but when they promise me a security and honor God has not promised, I have already begun to marvel."

### C7. 17.md, 默想 last paragraph: turn the marvel toward the Lamb
- Quoted: 「可是她不是同着巴比伦，而是同着羔羊。得胜的，是羔羊，和同着他的人。」
- Problem: the meditation rightly breaks the spell of Babylon. It never says what is worth marveling at, so the reader is left warned but not delighted. One sentence completes the 17:6 "marvel" thread with joy in Christ.
- Rewrite (insert between the two sentences):
  - zh: 「约翰曾在那女人面前大大地希奇；真正叫人希奇的，是这位借着被杀而得胜的羔羊。」
  - en: "John once marveled greatly at the woman; the One truly worth marveling at is the Lamb who conquered by being slain."
  - (If S2 option B is used, this sits just before 「得胜的，是羔羊……」, and 「借着被杀」 then echoes 5:9 there, so shorten to 「真正叫人希奇的，是这位羔羊。」 / "the One truly worth marveling at is this Lamb.")

### C8. 17.md, variety note: face as the motive
- Face/standing drives the 06 (丢脸), 14 (为了面子) and 17 (在亲戚面前抬得起头) meditations. In 17 it belongs to the scenario and fits 17:4, so keep it, but 18-22 should not reach for face again. Restitution (para 3) comes before the grace paragraph. It is already qualified (「她的盼望并不在于她能赔回多少」), and with S2 option A it is followed directly by the gospel ground, so no change is needed.

## What works and should be kept

- 16: the altar's "Yes" as the voice of the slain, not of safe onlookers, together with the honest turn "I do not stand only on the victims' side". This is grace that will not flatter the reader. The Gethsemane cup with Isa 51:22 and Rom 5:9 is the clearest substitution in the 06-22 meditations. "The pastor need not now switch to preaching by fear" rightly guards against overcorrecting. The Volf paragraph and the line about reporting to the police serve real victims well.
- 16 body: "Repentance is not squeezed out by suffering; it is given by God's grace" and "the Lord's instruction is not to calculate Armageddon but to stay awake, clothed in the white garments he gives". Both are gospel-shaped and pastoral for Chinese churches that have heard date-setting.
- 17: a sympathetic, concrete, current scenario (理财平台爆雷, the five-star launch, two widows). Separating the deceived from the deceivers is careful, and the church's duties (police, no 家丑不可外扬 cover-up, care for widows) are rightly put on the church. "Babylon has never loved her guests" lands strongly. "Called and chosen, and so faithful" (17:14) is the right indicative-before-imperative ground, and the ending "her place beside the Lamb began with his call, not with her success" should stay.
