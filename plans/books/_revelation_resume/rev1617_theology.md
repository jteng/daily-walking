## MUST-FIX

### M1. 16.md, 默想 / Meditation, last paragraph: ending repeats 15's scene
- Quote: 「七碗倒出来的时候，在基督里的人不在碗下。他们站在玻璃海上唱：「万世之王啊，你的道途义哉！诚哉！」（15:3）……」 / "They stand beside the sea of glass and sing, "Just and true are your ways, O King of the nations!" (15:3)..."
- Problem: 15's meditation ends in adoration on 15:3-4 by the sea of glass, so 16 lands on the same verse and scene. The brief assigns 16 to end on Christ who drank the cup (John 18:11). 15's meditation also already used the night of the betrayal (Mark 14:26), so 16's ending should stay on the cup itself, not go back to the sea.
- Fix: replace the whole last paragraph:
  - zh: 七碗倒出来的时候，在基督里的人不在碗下，不是因为那杯被倒掉了，而是因为它已经被喝干了。彼得拔刀，想替主挡住那一夜；主却叫他收刀，自己转身去接那杯（约18:11）。他本可以求父差遣十二营多天使来（太26:53），却没有求。所以今晚小雅可以重新打开第16章，读到「是的，主神－全能者啊，你的判断义哉！诚哉！」（16:7），也跟着说一声「是的」。她能这样说，不是因为她比从前勇敢，而是因为那本该递给她的杯，在那园子里，已经被那位说「我父所给我的那杯，我岂可不喝呢？」的主接过去了。
  - en: When the seven bowls are poured out, those who are in Christ are not under them. That is not because the cup was poured away, but because it was drunk dry. Peter drew his sword to shield his Lord that night; Jesus told him to put it away and turned to take the cup himself (John 18:11). He could have asked the Father for more than twelve legions of angels (Matt 26:53), and he did not ask. So tonight Xiaoya can open chapter 16 again, read "Yes, Lord God the Almighty, true and just are your judgments!" (16:7), and say her own "Yes." She can say it not because she is braver than before, but because the cup that should have been handed to her was taken, in that garden, by the Lord who said, "Shall I not drink the cup that the Father has given me?"

### M2. 17.md, 默想 / Meditation, para 2: a disputed reading used as fact
- Quote: 「连约翰看见那穿紫色朱红色、满身金子珍珠的女人，也「大大地希奇」。受迷惑的不只是贪心的人，忠心的人也会被这样的光彩震住。」 / "Even John ... "marveled greatly." It is not only the greedy who are dazzled; faithful people can be stunned by such splendor too."
- Problem: 17:1-6 point 5 rightly gives two readings of John's wonder (perplexity, or being dazzled). The meditation then builds on "dazzled" as settled. The application still works if both readings are kept open.
- Fix:
  - zh: 叫我们希奇的，往往不是赤裸裸的罪，而是金杯。约翰看见那穿紫色朱红色、满身金子珍珠的女人，也「大大地希奇」；不论他是困惑，还是一时被震住，天使都不让他停在那里。受吸引的不只是贪心的人，忠心的人也会被这样的光彩迷住。
  - en: What makes us marvel is usually not naked sin but a golden cup. John too, seeing the woman in purple and scarlet, covered in gold and pearls, "marveled greatly"; whether he was perplexed or for a moment dazzled, the angel would not leave him there. It is not only the greedy who are drawn in; faithful people can be dazzled by such splendor too.

- Problem: the ESV reads "five of whom have fallen, one is" (17:10). The script check flags it.

### M4. 16.md (en only), Going Deeper 16:17-21, point 4: wrong claim about the ESV word order

- Problem: in the CUV the verse ends 「人就亵渎神」, so the zh is right. The ESV ends "because the plague was so severe", so the English claim is false.
- Fix (en): "and yet the chapter closes with people who "cursed God for the plague of the hail" (16:21)."

## SHOULD-FIX

### S1. 16.md, Going Deeper 16:17-21, 要点 / The point: Trinitarian precision
- Quote: 「审判世界的神，也是在十字架上为信他的人担当了审判的神。」 / "The God who judges the world is the God who, on the cross, bore judgment for all who trust in him."
- Problem: in context "God" is the One on the throne (16:17). Saying the Judge himself "bore judgment on the cross" blurs Father and Son (it reads as patripassian). Say plainly that the Son, the Lamb, bore it.
- Fix:
  - zh: > 要点：七碗以宝座上的一句「成了」结束。审判世界的是神；在十字架上为信他的人担当这审判的，是神的儿子，那被杀的羔羊。
  - en: > The point: The bowls end with the word from the throne, "It is done!" The God who judges the world is the God who gave his Son; on the cross the Lamb bore that judgment for all who trust in him.

### S2. 16.md, Going Deeper 16:5-7, point 4, Volf bullet: overstated biography

### S3. 17.md, Going Deeper 17:7-11, point 4 (and 16.md 16:12 point 2): the Nero legend is credited to the wrong sources
- Quote: 「尼禄没有死，或者要复活，带着帕提亚人的军队回来；……（塔西佗《历史》第二卷第八章；苏埃托尼乌斯《尼禄传》第五十七章）」 / "that he had not died, or would rise again, and would return with Parthian armies ... (Tacitus, *Histories* 2.8; Suetonius, *Nero* 57)."
- Problem: Tacitus *Hist.* 2.8 (a false Nero in Achaia and Asia, AD 69) and Suetonius *Nero* 57 (Nero expected to return; a pretender about 20 years later backed by the Parthians) attest the "not dead, will return" form and Parthian support for a pretender. They do not attest Nero rising from the dead or leading Parthian armies back; those forms come from the *Sibylline Oracles* (books 4 and 5) and later texts (e.g. *Ascension of Isaiah* 4). Carry the same correction into 13.md, which cites the same two sources for "would return to life".
- Fix:
  - zh: 尼禄在主后68年自杀以后，罗马帝国东部流传一种说法：尼禄并没有死，还要回来；后来出现了几个冒充尼禄的人，其中一个还得到帕提亚人的支持（塔西佗《历史》第二卷第八章；苏埃托尼乌斯《尼禄传》第五十七章）。较晚的犹太和基督教作品，例如《西卜神谕》第四、五卷，更把这说法发展成尼禄要带着东方的军队回来，甚至从死里复活。
  - en: After Nero's suicide in AD 68, a story spread in the eastern empire that he had not really died and would return; several pretenders claiming to be Nero appeared, one of them backed by the Parthians (Tacitus, *Histories* 2.8; Suetonius, *Nero* 57). Later Jewish and Christian writings, such as books 4 and 5 of the *Sibylline Oracles*, developed it into Nero returning at the head of eastern armies, and even returning from the dead.
  - Keep the next sentence, and it may name Bauckham (*The Climax of Prophecy*, ch. on Nero) as a representative of "many scholars".

### S4. 17.md, Going Deeper 17:12-14, point 4: wrong proof text for "called"
- Quote: 「「蒙召」和「被选」是神的作为（参太22:14；罗8:30）」 / "(see Matt 22:14; Rom 8:30)"
- Problem: in Matt 22:14 "called" (*klētoi*) is the general invitation that many receive without being chosen, which is the opposite of the effectual sense the sentence needs (and which 17:14 has).
- Fix: zh 「（参罗8:30；彼前2:9）」; en "(see Rom 8:30; 1 Pet 2:9)". Optionally hedge the sentence before it: 「许多释经者指出，三个词的次序……」 / "Many note that the order of the three words matters..."

### S5. 17.md, Going Deeper 17:5, 18, "Papal Rome" bullet: Luther 1520 stated loosely
- Quote: 「马丁·路德1520年的著作《教会被掳于巴比伦》，就用了这个画面」 / "Martin Luther's 1520 treatise *On the Babylonian Captivity of the Church* used this picture"

- Fix:
  - zh: 马丁·路德1520年的著作《教会被掳于巴比伦》以以色列被掳到巴比伦比喻教会受教廷辖制，书中也直说教皇制就是巴比伦的国度；许多宗教改革家和后来的新教作者也这样读第17章。
  - en: Martin Luther's 1520 treatise *On the Babylonian Captivity of the Church* likened the church under the papacy to Israel in exile, and in it he called the papacy "the kingdom of Babylon"; many Reformers and later Protestant writers read chapter 17 this way.

The meditation's "two ledgers" and its ending on 17:14, "called and chosen" before "faithful", are grace-grounded and fit the assignment.
