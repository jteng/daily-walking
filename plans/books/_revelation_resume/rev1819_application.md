### S2. 18.md, 默想 / Meditation, paragraph 4: the remedy repeats Revelation 13's, and the manager's heart is never named
- **Location (zh):** 「也可能是在会上把真实的工时报上去，不再替公司掩盖。」 / (en) "or reporting the real hours at the meeting instead of covering for the company."
- **Problem (variety):** Revelation 11 (小吴 refuses to sign a failed inspection report) and Revelation 13 (小陈 refuses to sign the reports and tells "those who need to know") have already resolved on refusing to sign or reporting the truth to the company. A third version in 18 makes the workplace-integrity step feel stock. Workplace and money scenes are also now in 06, 11, 13, 17 and 18. The step that belongs to 18 alone is the one that reverses the sin: no longer passing the pressure down onto people as if they were cargo.
- **Problem (heart):** The meditation names what he does (treats people as figures) but not why he does it. The boss's line "plenty of others will" is the key, because Babylon rules him through fear of being replaceable. One sentence on that motive turns this from behaviour into an idol, and it makes the later "my people" land harder, because that is exactly the name he fears he lacks.
- **Rewrite, part 1:** in paragraph 1, after 「老板对他说过，"你不干，有的是人干。"」 / "...plenty of others will.'", add:
  - zh: 「他心里最怕的，就是有一天自己也成了那个随时可以被换掉的人。他越怕被当作成本，就越把别人当作成本。」
  - en: "What he fears most is becoming the one who can be replaced at any moment. The more he fears being treated as a cost, the more he treats others as costs."
- **Rewrite, part 2:** replace the quoted sentence with:
  - zh: 「也可能是把那个年轻人的活儿减下来，下一次上面再压指标的时候，不再原样压给手下的人，而是在会上说一句：『这个数字，我们做不到。』」
  - en: "or it may mean lightening the young man's load, and the next time targets come down from above, not pressing them straight onto his team but saying in the meeting, 'We cannot do this number.'"

### S3. 19.md, 默想 / Meditation, paragraph 2: the wedding-garment parable leaves a fearful reader with a new test
- **Location (zh):** 「许多释经者认为，他的问题不在于他穷，或者衣服不够体面，而在于他不把王的筵席当一回事，不肯照着王的意思来赴席。」 / (en) "Many interpreters think his problem was not that he was poor or that his clothes were not respectable, but that he took the king's feast lightly and would not come on the king's terms."
- **Problem:** The sister's fear is "the host will see through me." The paragraph answers that fear well and then ends on a condition, "would not come on the king's terms." A conscientious reader (the very reader this meditation is for) will now worry whether she has met those terms. The paragraph needs one more sentence saying that her fear is the opposite of that man's contempt, so that the parable lands as comfort and not as a second inspection.
- **Rewrite:** keep the quoted sentence and add straight after it:
  - zh: 「他的错，和这位姊妹正好相反：她怕自己不配，他却根本不在乎王。一个怕被看穿、空着手来到王面前求恩的人，正是王要给她衣服穿的人。」
  - en: "His fault is the opposite of hers: she fears she is not fit, and he did not care about the king at all. Someone who is afraid of being seen through, and comes to the king empty-handed asking for mercy, is exactly the one the king means to clothe."

## Consider

### C1. 18.md, 默想 / Meditation, paragraph 5: let the victim hear justice in the same breath as the oppressor's welcome
- **Location (zh):** 「好叫那些被当作货物的人，和那些把人当作货物的人，都可以因信被神称为自己的百姓。」 / (en) "so that both those treated as cargo and those who treated them so could be called, through faith, God's own people."
- **Problem:** This is true gospel. But a reader who is the collapsed young man may hear it as "your boss gets the same welcome, and what happened to you does not matter." The chapter's own answer, that God remembers (18:5) and heaven rejoices when the wronged are vindicated (18:20), is in paragraph 3 but not here.
- **Rewrite:** add after the sentence:
  - zh: 「这不是说那个年轻人受的苦可以一笔勾销：神记得（18:5），十字架也正是神对这种罪的审判，并没有轻轻放过。」
  - en: "This does not mean the young man's suffering is simply written off: God remembers (18:5), and the cross is God's judgment on this very sin, not a way of letting it pass."

### C2. 18.md, 默想 / Meditation: no note of joy
- **Problem:** The chapter has heaven's rejoicing (18:20) and the coming wedding music (18:23 into 19:7), and the 深读 brings both out well. The meditation is accurate and warm, but its only affection is conviction. A single clause near the end would let the reader delight in the God who calls and not only obey him. This is optional; the ending on 「我的民哪」 already lands.
- **Rewrite:** in the last paragraph, after 「巴比伦要倾倒，她的账本要被烧尽；」 / "Babylon will fall and her ledgers will burn;", insert:
  - zh: 「她城里的琴声和婚礼的声音都要止息，羔羊的婚筵却要开席；」
  - en: "her harps and wedding songs will fall silent, while the Lamb's wedding feast begins;"

### C3. 18.md and 17.md: the same "what comes first" pivot in two chapters running
- **Location:** 18: 「可是这一节经文最先说的，不是「出来」，而是「我的民哪」。」 17: 「「蒙召」和「被选」在先，「有忠心」在后。」 (22 also opens its pivot with 「先看这两节经文的次序」.)
- **Problem:** Readers going straight through 17, 18 and 19 will hear the same move three times (see C5 for 19). In 18 the word order is the text's own and carries the chapter, so keep it. Just avoid flagging it as an ordering device a second time.
- **Rewrite (optional, light):** zh: 「可是在说「出来」以前，神先叫了一声：「我的民哪」。」 / en: "But before God says 'come out,' he calls them by name: 'my people.'"

### C4. 19.md, 默想 / Meditation: given white clothing repeats Revelation 03 and 00
- **Problem:** The Revelation 03 meditation already resolves on white garments that Christ gives and pays for (3:18, Isa 61:10, John 19:23-24), and 00 uses 7:14. 19's borrowed-dress scene and its fear of exposure are new and strong, so the scenario should stay. But the resolution should lean on what only 19 has: the invitation (19:9) and the Bridegroom's joy (19:7), not a second "the robe is given" sermon. C5 and C6 do this without adding length.

### C5. 19.md, 默想 / Meditation, paragraph 2: "begins with an invitation, not..." repeats Revelation 17's phrase
- **Location (zh):** 「这个筵席是从邀请开始的，不是从检查开始的。」 / (en) "This feast begins with an invitation, not an inspection."
- **Problem:** Revelation 17 ends 「是从他的呼召开始的，不是从她的成功开始的」. The two adjacent chapters use the same sentence frame. It also sits uneasily beside the parable that follows, where the king does come in to look.
- **Rewrite:**
  - zh: 「天使吩咐约翰写下的，是一张请帖，不是一张检查单。」
  - en: "What the angel tells John to write is an invitation, not a checklist."

### C6. 19.md, 默想 / Meditation, last paragraph: make Cana do its work and close the childhood image in joy
- **Location (zh):** 「可是他也是那曾经在加利利的迦拿被请去赴席、坐在乡村婚宴上的主（约2:1-2）。请帖上写的话是真实的，因为写请帖的那一位，他的名字就叫诚信真实。」
- **Problem:** Cana is a fine choice, but as written it is only a fact about Jesus. At Cana he quietly spared a poor family's shame when the wine ran out (John 2:3, 9-10), which is exactly this sister's childhood fear. The ending also stays on "the invitation is reliable." That is close to the "the Lord is faithful" endings already in 1 and 2 Thessalonians, and it never returns to the chapter's centre, which is joy and glory given to God (19:7). Her scene opened with a child who "hardly dared to reach for food"; the meditation can close by letting her eat and rejoice.
- **Rewrite** (replaces from 「可是他也是……」 to the end):
  - zh: 「可是他也是那曾经在加利利的迦拿，坐在一场乡村婚宴上的主（约2:1-2）。那天酒用尽了，主人家眼看要丢脸，他没有当众揭穿谁，只是悄悄地把水变成了好酒（约2:9-10）。请她赴席的，就是这一位。那一天，她不必再缩着手、不敢夹菜；她可以和众圣徒一同欢喜快乐，将荣耀归给他（19:7），因为请帖上的那位新郎，名字就叫诚信真实。」
  - en: "Yet he is also the Lord who once sat at a village wedding in Cana of Galilee (John 2:1-2). When the wine ran out and the family was about to be shamed, he exposed no one; he quietly turned water into the best wine (John 2:9-10). This is the one who has invited her. On that day she will not have to hold back her hand at the table; with all the saints she may rejoice and exult and give him the glory (19:7), because the Bridegroom named on the invitation is called Faithful and True."

## What works well (keep)

- **18:** The manager is "both on the list and writing it." This is honest about sin without crushing him, and it turns the cargo list (18:13) into something a reader can recognise in their own working week. Grace comes before command: "my people" before "come out", and "not wash ourselves first before we may come". The practical steps come only after that ground is laid. The line for people who are themselves being exploited (labour law, leaving a place that devours people "is not unfaithfulness") is pastorally important; keep it. The thirty pieces of silver as a slave's price (Exod 21:32; Phil 2:7) is a striking Christ-centred turn that belongs to this chapter alone, and the ending puts the reader's eyes on God naming his people, not on the reader's own resolve. Exod 3:7 is a good, unused anchor for "God sees those treated as cargo."
- **19:** The borrowed, oversized dress at a cousin's wedding is concrete, Chinese, and new in the plan. It ties face, poverty and fear of exposure to the text exactly. The sins named (coldness to a mother-in-law, lies at work, years without the Bible) are specific, ordinary and honest. Paragraph 3 is a model of indicative before imperative: "not to earn a garment... because she is already wearing a garment she will never have to give back", with Phil 2:13. The 深读 on "granted her" and the order of blood, gift and deeds gives the meditation solid ground. The handling of 19:21, where the solemn judgment turns to the 22:17 invitation and the blood shed "is the pledge that he is willing to receive them", lands well.
