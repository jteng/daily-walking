## SHOULD-FIX

### A1. 21.md, 默想 / Meditation paragraph 3: the "free water to the thirsty, don't mend yourself first" pivot duplicates 22's pivot, and God himself as the gift is missing

- zh: 「生命泉的水是「白白」赐给口渴的人的。……不是先把自己修好了再来，而是口渴的时候就来。」
- en: "The water of the spring of life is given 'without payment' to the thirsty. ... Not after first mending himself, but when he is thirsty."
- Problem: (1) Repetition. 22's meditation turns on the same move: "The Lord does not first require us... He gives the water first, freely, to the thirsty" (22:17), and its resolution is 「主啊，我愿意先来喝」. Readers will hear the same pivot in the last two chapters of the whole plan (and 07 already ends on "springs of living water"). 22 has the stronger claim to it, since 22:17 is its key verse. (2) God as the goal. Xiulan's hope in 21 is laid out as a restored body, family and land, grounded in the cross. That is good, but the chapter's own centre, "God himself will be with them" (21:3) and "I will be his God" (21:7), never reaches her. The heir receives God himself, as the exposition says at 21:7. Her father walked away, and 21:7 (with 2 Cor 6:18) speaks straight to that wound. Without it the meditation risks making the new creation the prize and God the one who supplies it. A clause is enough, since 22 gives the full "see his face" climax.
- Suggested rewrite. Replace paragraph 3 up to 「也许秀兰今天能做的」; the rest of the paragraph stays as it is.
- zh:
  「这一切的根据，不在秀兰身上。坐宝座的接着说：「都成了！」（21:6）羔羊已经在十字架上成就了救赎，所以神能宣告新创造已经成就。他又说：「我要作他的神，他要作我的儿子。」（21:7）保罗把同一个应许用在信徒身上，说得更亲切：「我要作你们的父；你们要作我的儿女。」（林后6:18）秀兰的父亲一走就是好几年，音信全无；坐宝座的那一位却说，他要亲自作她的父。比重新站起来的双腿更好的，比重建的村子更好的，是「神要亲自与他们同在」（21:3）。这一切都是白白赐的：对神说过难听话的人可以来，对母亲说过伤人话的人也可以来，连那位欠了债、一走了之的父亲，也可以来，不必先把自己修好。也许秀兰今天能做的，……」
- en:
  "None of this rests on Xiulan. The One on the throne goes on: "It is done!" (21:6). The Lamb has accomplished redemption on the cross, and so God can declare the new creation accomplished. Then he says, "I will be his God and he will be my son" (21:7). Paul applies the same promise to believers more tenderly still: "I will be a father to you, and you shall be sons and daughters to me" (2 Cor 6:18). Xiulan's father walked away years ago and has not been heard from; the One on the throne says he himself will be her Father. Better than legs that walk again, better than a village rebuilt, is this: "God himself will be with them" (21:3). And all of it is given without payment. The one who has said ugly things to God may come; the one who has said wounding things to her mother may come; even the father who ran up debts and walked away may come, without first mending himself. Perhaps all Xiulan can do today..."
- Notes: 2 Cor 6:18 checked with bible_lookup (CUV 「我要作你们的父；你们要作我的儿女。这是全能的主说的。」 / ESV "and I will be a father to you, and you shall be sons and daughters to me, says the Lord Almighty"). It is not in WORKFLOW's used lists. The edit adds roughly 150 characters and removes the thirst sentences, so the length is about the same. The 21:6 / Isa 55:1 "without payment" exposition stays in 深读 21:5-8, which is fine.

## CONSIDER

### A2. 21.md, Meditation paragraph 1 / 3: treat her "ugly words to God" as lament as well as sin
- zh: 「她也承认，这几年她对神说过很多难听的话」 and later 「对神说过难听话的人可以来」.
- Problem: For a woman in a wheelchair whose family fell apart, some of those words are probably the honest complaint the Psalms make room for. If they are counted only as sin to be forgiven, a suffering reader can hear that her grief offended God. The draft already leans the right way ("tell God that honest sentence just as it is"). One clause makes it explicit.
- Suggested addition after 「只是把那句实话原原本本地告诉神」: zh 「（诗篇里不少祷告，就是这样对神说的实话）」; en after "tell God that honest sentence just as it is": "(many of the psalms are prayers just that honest)". No new reference is needed.

### A3. 21.md, Meditation last paragraph: the Lamb-as-lamp line is said three times
- zh: 「照亮那城的……而是羔羊。……那一天，她要用复活的身体，在羔羊的光中行走（21:24）。那里没有黑夜，因为羔羊就是那城的灯。」
- Problem: The quotation, the 「照亮那城的」 sentence and the last sentence all say the same thing, so the ending loses some force. The Matt 27:45 move (he entered the darkness so that she need not stay in it) is the heart of the paragraph and should stay. The brief asks for the ending to be "there will be no night there", which can land on the resurrection-walk image.
- Suggested last sentences: zh 「那一天，她要用复活的身体，在羔羊的光中行走（21:24）；「在那里原没有黑夜」（21:25）。」 en "On that day she will walk in the Lamb's light in her risen body (21:24), and 'there will be no night there' (21:25)." Keep the 「照亮那城的，不是它的碧玉，也不是它的精金」 sentence: it quietly answers the gold-and-jewels contrast with the Pure Land and the prosperity gospel.

### A4. 22.md, Meditation paragraph 3: the "Come" to her parents should not become one more burden she carries alone
- zh: 「她可以向主说「来」，也可以向父母说「来」。这两个「来」，在同一节经文里。」
- en: "She can say 'Come' to the Lord, and she can say 'Come' to her parents. Both are in the same verse."
- Problem: A 26-year-old whose reluctance comes partly from love for unbelieving parents may hear this as a task. The verse puts the Spirit first: she says "Come" with him, not in his place. Saying so keeps the parents' salvation in God's hands. It also affirms that her concern for them is not a lack of longing for Christ but shares the heart of the Spirit who is still inviting.
- Suggested rewrite: zh 「她可以向主说「来」，也可以向父母说「来」；她不是独自去说，先说「来」的是圣灵。这两个「来」，在同一节经文里。」 en "She can say 'Come' to the Lord, and she can say 'Come' to her parents; she does not say it alone, for the first to say 'Come' is the Spirit. Both are in the same verse."

## What works well and should be kept

- **21:** Renewal against escape, aimed at a real Chinese church habit (「这世界非我家」 sung loudest by the one with the most to escape). The hymn is affirmed ("that is right") before it is corrected, so no one is shamed for loving it. Xiulan's honest sentence ("fish me out of a heap of ruins") is the best line in the chapter. The resurrection body as "Xiulan herself" (Luke 24:39; 1 Cor 15:43) speaks to a disabled reader without sentimentality. The twelve sons of Jacob on the gates, a family of favouritism and hatred carved into God's city, is fresh and full of grace, and it invites without promising that every family member will be there. "None of this rests on Xiulan" gives gospel ground before the one small step (say sorry to her mother), and the step is followed at once by "renewal does not begin with her effort". The Matt 27:45 ending (he entered the darkness so she need not live in night) puts Christ as Saviour at the close, as assigned, with no tears (07) and no morning star (00).
- **21 exposition:** the 21:8 "cowardly" warning held together with Peter's restoration and 1 Cor 6:11; the prosperity-gospel and Pure Land correctives point to God and the Lamb, not to gold; the cube as the Most Holy Place makes God's presence the wonder of the city.
- **22:** The meditation is honest about two kinds of not-longing: the young believer who does not want the Lord to come yet, and the leader whose heart has gone dry. The leader confesses first, so there is no guilt without hope. The pivot is built on the actual order of 22:17 before 22:20: the Lord gives the water before he asks for the longing. That is grace before imperative drawn straight from the text. "The good things Xiao Zhou cannot bear to lose are light that shines from that face" moves her toward God as the treasure, the most God-centred line in the two chapters, without despising marriage, work or parents (21:26). 「主啊，我愿意先来喝」 is true to the text and pastorally wise. The ending ("not a test to pass... a blessing", 22:21) closes the whole plan on grace and on Christ, and it does not end on the morning star.
- **22 exposition:** 22:13 as the book's Christological climax; 22:14 explained on the robes washed in the Lamb's blood; the 22:18-19 warning balanced with the faithful-pastor line; "the church is not left only with fear. It opens its mouth and asks the Lord to come."
