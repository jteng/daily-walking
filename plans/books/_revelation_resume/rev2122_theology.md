## MUST-FIX

### M1. 21.md, 深读 21:9-21 point 4: CUV misquote of 18:16
- zh: 「巴比伦用来夸耀的，正是「金子、宝石、珍珠」（18:16）」
- Problem: CUV reads 「金子、宝石，和珍珠」. The checker passed it because it splits on 、, but 「金子、宝石、珍珠」 is not CUV wording. (English "gold, with jewels, and with pearls" is correct ESV.)
- Fix (zh): 「巴比伦用来夸耀的，正是「金子、宝石，和珍珠」（18:16）。」

### M2. 22.md, 深读 22:1-5 point 4: Trinitarian wording that can read as modalism
- zh: 「许多释经者认为，这表示父与羔羊合而为一，同受敬拜，却不是两位神。」
- en: "Many interpreters take this to show that the Father and the Lamb are one, worshiped together, and yet not two gods."
- Problem: "the Father and the Lamb are one... not two gods" with no word for their distinctness can be heard as the Father and the Lamb being one person. That is the very confusion Oneness and Eastern Lightning teaching trades on, in this Chinese setting. The text itself keeps them distinct ("God *and* the Lamb"), and the singular pronouns show the one God. Both halves need saying.
- Fix (zh): 「许多释经者认为，经文一面说「神和羔羊」，分明是两位；一面又用单数的「他」，表示父与羔羊同坐一个宝座，同受敬拜，同是一位神，而不是两位神。」
- Fix (en): "Many interpreters take this to show that the Father and the Lamb, though distinct ('God and the Lamb'), share one throne, receive one worship and are one God, not two gods."

### M3. 22.md, 深读 22:12-17 point 3: 22:14 manuscript evidence overstated and mislabelled
- zh: 「许多较早的抄本作「洗净自己衣服」……许多较晚的抄本作遵守他诫命的」
- en: "many earlier manuscripts read 'wash their robes'... many later manuscripts read 'do his commandments'"
- Problem: Only a few early Greek witnesses survive for Revelation, so "many earlier manuscripts" is factually loose. The reading "wash their robes" (*plynontes tas stolas autōn*) rests chiefly on Codex Sinaiticus and Codex Alexandrinus (4th-5th c.) with the Vulgate. "Do his commandments" (*poiountes tas entolas autou*) is the reading of most later (Byzantine) manuscripts, followed by the Textus Receptus and the KJV. The "look alike in Greek" point is sound (Metzger's *Textual Commentary* makes it), so keep it.
- Fix (zh): 「这里有一个经文异文：最古老的抄本（如西奈抄本、亚历山大抄本）作「洗净自己衣服」，和合本和英文标准版都照这个读法；后来大多数的抄本作遵守他诫命的，英文钦定本就是照这个读法译的。两种读法在希腊文中写起来很相近。」
- Fix (en): "There is a textual variant here: the oldest manuscripts (such as Codex Sinaiticus and Codex Alexandrinus) read 'wash their robes,' which the Chinese Union Version and the ESV follow; most later manuscripts read 'do his commandments,' which the King James Version translates. In Greek the two readings look much alike."

## SHOULD-FIX

### S1. 21.md, 深读 21:9-21 point 2: the "one people" reading stated as settled fact
- zh: 「旧约的百姓和新约的教会，一同成为一座城。神的百姓从来只有一个，就是因信归于基督的人。」
- en: "The Old Testament people and the New Testament church together form one city. God has only ever had one people: those who belong to Christ by faith."
- Problem: This is the covenantal and majority reading, and a good one. But the brief names dispensational representatives throughout, and dispensationalists (e.g. Walvoord) read the tribes and apostles as Israel and the church kept distinct inside the one city. Present it as this study's reading.
- Fix (zh): 「……一同成为一座城。许多释经者认为，这表示神的百姓从来只有一个，就是因信归于基督的人；时代论的释经者（例如约翰·华尔伏）则认为，以色列和教会在这城里同住，却仍各有分别。无论哪一种，两者都因羔羊得以进城。」
- Fix (en): "...together form one city. Many interpreters take this to mean God has only ever had one people, those who belong to Christ by faith; dispensational interpreters (e.g. John Walvoord) see Israel and the church sharing the city while remaining distinct. On either reading, both enter through the Lamb."

### S2. 21.md, 深读 21:9-21 point 1: the "both" view is attributed to Beale alone
- zh: 「两样都是：许多释经者（例如毕尔）认为……」 / en: "Both: Many interpreters (for example Beale)..."
- Problem: Beale (NIGTC, 1999) reads the city chiefly as a figurative portrait of the people of God in his presence, set within a real new creation. He does not describe a literal city-place. What the draft says *about* him is fair, but calling this "both" stretches him. Osborne (BECNT, 2002) states the "both a people and a place" position more directly. The Gundry attribution is accurate ("The New Jerusalem: People as Place, not Place for People," *NovT* 29, 1987). So is Walvoord, who takes it as a literal city.
- Fix (zh): 「两样都是：许多释经者（例如格兰特·奥斯本）认为，这城既是神的百姓，也是一个真实的地方；毕尔也强调，这城主要描写神的百姓和神的同在，却是在真实的「新地」上（21:1-2），不是飘在空中的一种心境。」(If 奥斯本 already appears earlier in the chapter, his full name goes at that first mention. It currently first appears at 21:8, which comes earlier, so write 奥斯本 here.)
- Fix (en): "Both: Many interpreters (e.g. Osborne) hold that the city is both a people and a place; Beale likewise stresses that it chiefly portrays God's people and his presence, yet set on a real 'new earth' (21:1-2), not a state of mind floating in the air."

### S3. 21.md, 深读 21:9-21 point 4: OT background for the jewelled city is missing
- Problem: The section goes straight to Exod 28 and Babylon. The direct prophetic source, Isa 54:11-12 (foundations of sapphires, gates of carbuncles, a wall of precious stones, for afflicted Zion), is never named. The review rules weight OT background first, and this verse also sharpens the contrast with the Pure Land paragraph (S4).
- Fix (zh), add before 「许多人指出」: 「以赛亚早已应许那受困苦的锡安：「我必以彩色安置你的石头，以蓝宝石立定你的根基」（赛54:11）。」
- Fix (en): "Isaiah had already promised afflicted Zion, 'I will set your stones in antimony, and lay your foundations with sapphires' (Isa 54:11)."
- Run bible_lookup 23:54:11,12 and quote verbatim; trim to fit.

### S4. 21.md, 深读 21:1-4 point 2: the Amitabha Sutra comparison needs its difference stated
- zh: 「……和《阿弥陀经》所讲的西方极乐世界差不多，那里也是黄金为地，有七宝池。」
- Problem: The sutra description is accurate (黄金为地, 七宝池). But Revelation also has gold and jewels, so a reader may conclude the two pictures are alike and the difference is only direction. Say what the real difference is: the centre of the city is God and the Lamb, entered by grace, not a paradise reached by rebirth through merit or reciting a name. Its imagery comes from Isaiah and Ezekiel, not from Buddhism.
- Fix (zh), add after 七宝池: 「启示录的城也有精金宝石，但它的画面来自以赛亚和以西结；它的中心不是金子，而是神和羔羊，人进去也不是靠念佛往生，而是白白蒙恩。」
- Fix (en): "Revelation's city also has gold and jewels, but its imagery comes from Isaiah and Ezekiel; its centre is not the gold but God and the Lamb, and entry is not by rebirth through reciting a name but by free grace."
- To keep the length down, cut the clause 「它提醒我们不要贪恋世界……这是对的」 to one short sentence.

### S5. 22.md, 深读 22:12-17 point 5: "Root of David" overstated against 05
- zh: 「他是「大卫的根」，就是大卫的源头和主」 / en: "He is 'the root' of David, his source and Lord"
- Problem: 05 (published) gives the main reading as Davidic-Messiah language from Isa 11:1, 10 (Aune, Mounce), with "some also see" David's son as David's Lord. 22 now states "source and Lord" as the plain meaning. Keep the two chapters consistent.
- Fix (zh): 「他是「大卫的根」，又是「他的后裔」：许多释经者认为这两个称号都强调他是从大卫家出来的弥赛亚君王（参赛11:1、10；见第5章），也有人看出，大卫的子孙同时是大卫的主（可12:35-37）。」
- Fix (en): "'The root and the descendant of David': many interpreters take both titles to stress that he is the Davidic Messiah-King (see Isa 11:1, 10; chapter 5), and some also see that David's son is David's Lord (Mark 12:35-37)."

### S6. 22.md, 深读 22:18-21 point 2: WCF 1.6 paraphrased loosely; "the church has long confessed"
- zh: 「教会也一直相信，神在圣经中已经把救恩所需要的一切都启示出来了（《威斯敏斯德信条》第一章第六节）。」
- Problem: WCF 1.6 (1646) is wider and more pointed than "all needed for salvation". It covers "the whole counsel of God concerning all things necessary for his own glory, man's salvation, faith and life", and adds "unto which nothing at any time is to be added, whether by new revelations of the Spirit, or traditions of men". That last clause is exactly the "adding" danger the next bullet names. "The church has long confessed" also turns one Reformed confession into the whole church. The confession is a fair source for this application, but 22:18-19 is not its proof text for this point, so don't imply it is.
- Fix (zh): 「……改革宗教会的《威斯敏斯德信条》（1646年）也承认：神关于他自己的荣耀、人的得救、信仰和生活所必需的全部旨意，都已记在圣经中，或可从圣经中正当推论出来；「无论是借着圣灵的新启示，或是人的遗传，任何时候都不可再加添什么」（第一章第六节）。」(Quote marks are fine for a confession, but do not use 「」 if the checker will treat it as CUV. Use plain wording or 《》 per house style.)
- Fix (en): "...and the Reformed churches confess in the *Westminster Confession of Faith* (1646) that the whole counsel of God concerning all things necessary for his glory, man's salvation, faith and life is set down in Scripture or deducible from it, 'unto which nothing at any time is to be added, whether by new revelations of the Spirit, or traditions of men' (1.6)."

### S7. 22.md, 深读 22:12-17 point 2: overlooks 1:17 and 2:8
- zh: 「现在说这话的，是那位「必快来」的耶稣。」 / en: "Now the speaker is Jesus, who 'is coming soon.'"
- Problem: As written, this sounds like the first time Jesus takes the divine title. He already said "I am the first and the last" in 1:17 and 2:8. The climax is that he now takes *all three* pairs, including Alpha-Omega, which 1:8 and 21:6 gave to the Lord God. Saying this makes the deity argument stronger and more accurate.
- Fix (zh): 「主耶稣在1:17和2:8已经说过「我是首先的，我是末后的」；现在，那位「必快来」的耶稣把三组称号，连同主神在1:8和21:6所用的「阿拉法、俄梅戛」，都用在自己身上。」
- Fix (en): "Jesus already called himself 'the first and the last' (1:17; 2:8); now the Jesus who 'is coming soon' takes all three pairs to himself, including the 'Alpha and Omega' that the Lord God used in 1:8 and 21:6."

### S8. 22.md, 深读 22:1-5 point 2: "open to all"
- zh: 「生命树却在河的两边，人人都可以去。」 / en: "it stands on both sides of the river, open to all."
- Problem: Read with "healing of the nations", this sounds universalist. 22:14-15 limit access to those who wash their robes. The section on 21:24 makes the same guard; make it here too.
- Fix (zh): 「……生命树却在河的两边，向神所有的百姓敞开（参22:14）。」
- Fix (en): "...it stands on both sides of the river, open to all God's people (see 22:14)."

### S9. 22.md, 深读 22:18-21 point 3: Erasmus wording slightly imprecise
- Problem: The substance is right (Metzger, *Text of the NT* and *Textual Commentary*). Erasmus's 1516 edition used one Revelation manuscript (now minuscule 2814), which lacked 22:16-21. He back-translated from the Vulgate, and "book of life" came from Latin copies that read *libro* for *ligno* (a Latin scribal slip). It then passed through the Textus Receptus into the KJV. "缺了最后几节" is fine, but the reader should know the Latin reading was itself an error, otherwise it looks like a Greek rival reading.
- Fix (zh): 「……他便从拉丁文武加大译本译回希腊文；而他手上的拉丁文抄本，正好把「树」（ligno）误抄成了「册」（libro）。这个读法就这样进入了后来的英文钦定本所根据的经文。」
- Fix (en): "...so he translated them back into Greek from the Latin Vulgate, and the Latin copies he used had miscopied 'tree' (*ligno*) as 'book' (*libro*). This is how that reading came into the text behind the King James Version."
- Keep "Bruce Metzger and others note" as the hedge.

## CONSIDER

C1. **22.md 22:21 variants.** Add the Sinaiticus reading "with the saints" (μετὰ τῶν ἁγίων). zh: 「……也有的作「与圣徒同在」或与你们众人同在。」 en: "...'with the saints,' and 'with you all.'" ESV "with all" follows Alexandrinus. Your summary ("several readings; on any reading the last word is grace") is accurate.

C2. **22.md 22:17 meditation.** 「在主来以前，圣灵和新妇向他说「来」，也向口渴的人说「来」」 assumes reading 1 from the exposition. Add 「照许多释经者的理解」 / "as many read it", or keep it as is because the meditation's point holds on both readings.

C3. **22.md 22:17 options** have no named representatives (the brief asks for names). For reading 1 you could name Beale / Osborne / Mounce, who take the first two "Come"s as addressed to Christ. I am not confident of a named modern advocate for reading 2, so leave "也有释经者 / other interpreters" rather than guess.

C4. **21.md 21:22 point 1.** Ezek 48:35 is apt, but the stronger contrast is that Ezekiel's restored city gives nine chapters to its temple (Ezek 40-48), while John's city has none. One clause: 「以西结所见的复兴异象用了九章描写圣殿（结40-48章）」.

C5. **21.md 21:7.** Note that Revelation turns 2 Sam 7:14's "father" into "God" ("I will be his God"), blending it with the covenant formula of 21:3. One clause, both languages.

C6. **21.md 21:3.** Some early manuscripts (Sinaiticus, Alexandrinus) read "peoples" (*laoi*, plural; see the ESV footnote), which ties in with "the nations" of 21:24. Optional; skip it if you need the space.

C7. **22.md 22:12.** 「人得救，是因为名字写在羔羊的生命册上」: add 「本乎恩，也因着信」 / "by grace through faith" so the ground is plain and not only the record.

C8. **22.md The point at 22:18-21.** 「因为它已经把基督完全地启示出来了」 is good. Heb 1:1-2 would ground it ("in these last days he has spoken to us by his Son"), if you have room.

C9. **21.md 21:27 point 4** ends 「名字写在上面的根据，是羔羊为他们被杀」. That is close to 20's meditation ending ("because the Lamb was slain... written there by grace"). It is exposition, not 21's meditation ending, so it is allowed, but cutting it to a pointer avoids the echo (see cut 21-c).

Xiao Zhou's 「主啊，我愿意先来喝」 follows the order of 22:17 before 22:20. That is exegetically sharp and pastorally right.
