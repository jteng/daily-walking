## Shared interpretive framework (all files must agree; already set in 00)
- Author: John, who names himself (1:1, 4, 9); this study takes the traditional view that he is the apostle John (Justin Martyr, *Dialogue* 81; Irenaeus). Note Dionysius of Alexandria's doubts on style (Eusebius, *Church History* 7.25) as the main alternative ("John the elder" or another John). Keep consistent with 1 John 00 / 2 John 00.
- Date: most scholars c. AD 95 under Domitian (Irenaeus, *Against Heresies* 5.30.3); some argue c. AD 68 under Nero. Present both; this study follows the later date but nothing in chs. 1-5 depends on it. Don't overstate Domitian's persecution (evidence for an empire-wide persecution is debated; local pressure, the imperial cult in Asia, synagogue conflict).
- Genre: apocalypse, prophecy and letter at once (1:1-4). Symbols drawn mainly from the OT (Daniel, Ezekiel, Zechariah, Isaiah, Exodus). Numbers are symbolic in many readings.
- Four approaches, labelled symmetrically with representatives: preterist (mainly fulfilled in the first century), historicist (church history in sequence; many Reformers), futurist (mostly chs. 4-22 still future; dispensationalists and many historic premillennialists), idealist/spiritual (timeless conflict). This study reads eclectically, as many evangelical commentators do (e.g. G. K. 毕尔 Beale, 包衡 Bauckham, Grant Osborne): written to seven real churches, speaking to the church in every age, and looking to Christ's real, bodily return and the new creation.
- Millennium (ch. 20) is not settled here: name amillennial (e.g. Anthony Hoekema 安东尼·胡克玛), historic premillennial (George Eldon Ladd 乔治·赖德), dispensational premillennial, postmillennial — only in 00, briefly.
- Chinese church context: Revelation misused by date-setters and cults (东方闪电（自称全能神教会）claims about the sealed scroll), 666 speculation, fear-driven preaching; also churches under pressure (Smyrna, Philadelphia). Faithful-pastor balance wherever false teachers appear. Safeguarding wording (WORKFLOW) wherever abusive leaders or sexual exploitation come up (e.g. "Jezebel," 2:20).
- Debated points to present as options with names: 1:4 "seven spirits" (Holy Spirit via Isa 11:2/Zech 4, or seven angels); 1:10 "Lord's day" (Sunday; or day of the Lord); 1:20 "angels" of the churches (angels, messengers/leaders, or the church's personified spirit); the seven churches as successive church ages (Scofield-style dispensational reading) vs historical churches speaking to all churches; 4:1 "Come up here" (dispensational: picture of the rapture; others: John's visionary call); 24 elders (representing OT and NT people of God, or angelic beings); 4 living creatures (Ezek 1; Isa 6); 5:1 scroll (God's plan of judgment and redemption; covenant/title-deed reading; etc.).
- Names in ::zh: 包衡, 毕尔, 乔治·赖德, 安东尼·胡克玛, 优西比乌《教会史》, 爱任纽《驳异端》, 游斯丁; 英文钦定本, 英文标准版, 七十士译本, 不定过去时. Prose spells CUV names (拔摩, 以弗所, 士每拿, 别迦摩, 推雅推喇, 撒狄, 非拉铁非, 老底嘉, 尼哥拉一党, 安提帕, 耶洗别).


- Book-wide ::zh names (WORKFLOW "Revelation 00-05" note): 约翰·华尔伏, 《司可福串注圣经》, 毕尔, 包衡, 大卫·奥尼, 肯尼斯·詹特里, 格兰特·奥斯本 (full name at each chapter's first mention), 乔治·赖德, 安东尼·胡克玛, 威廉·亨德里克森, 罗兰·贝特纳（Loraine Boettner）, 多米田, 理想派, 历史前千禧年派, 本研读 (not 本书), 释经者, 不定过去时.
- Structure (from 00): seals (6-8:1), trumpets (8:2-11), bowls (15-16) — present recapitulation/progressive parallelism (Victorinus, Hendriksen, Beale) and sequential/telescoping futurist readings (Walvoord; Ladd/Osborne mixed) symmetrically, once per series; later chapters point back.
- Chapter-specific debated points to set out as options with named representatives, briefly:
  - 6: rider on the white horse (Christ/gospel vs conquest/antichrist; cf. 19:11); 6:9-11 martyrs. (Rev 6:10-11 "How long" is already used in a 2 Peter 03 meditation: expound it, but do not build 06's meditation on it.)
  - 7: 144,000 and the great multitude (same people seen two ways — Bauckham/Beale; ethnic Israel — Walvoord); Jehovah's Witnesses' 144,000 claim answered; 7:14 "great tribulation".
  - 8-9: trumpets and Exodus plagues; locusts and Abaddon/Apollyon; historicist readings (Saracens/Turks) named fairly; 9:20-21 no repentance.
  - 10-11: little scroll (Ezek 3); measuring the temple; 42 months / 1260 days / time, times, half a time; two witnesses (the witnessing church — Beale, Bauckham; two literal prophets, Moses/Elijah — Walvoord; preterist Jerusalem); 11:15 seventh trumpet.
  - 12: woman (people of God / Israel and church; Catholic Marian reading named fairly); war in heaven and the cross (12:10-11, John 12:31); 1260 days.
  - 13: beasts from sea and land; mark (counterfeit of the 7:3 seal; economic pressure); 666 — Nero Caesar gematria (Hebrew spelling) and the 616 variant (Irenaeus AH 5.30.1), symbolic "short of seven" reading, warn against date/number speculation (point to 00 for 东方闪电); historicist papal reading (Reformers; Westminster Confession 25.6 as originally worded) stated fairly and historically, without polemic.
  - 14: 144,000 on Zion; three angels; 14:13; harvest and winepress.
  - 15-16: song of Moses and the Lamb; bowls; Armageddon (16:16: Megiddo, literal battle vs symbolic final conflict).
  - 17-18: Babylon (Rome — most; apostate Jerusalem — Gentry/Chilton; papal Rome — historicists; future literal city — some dispensationalists); seven heads/kings (17:9-11; Nero redivivus legend, hedge); 18:4 "come out"; 18:13 "human souls".
  - 19: marriage supper; Rider "Faithful and True"; 19:15 sword from mouth; the battle; 19:10 worship God (used in 00 meditation; do not rebuild).
  - 20: the main place for the millennium. Set out amillennial (Augustine, Hoekema; 20:1-6 now, recapitulating ch. 19), historic premillennial (Ladd; also early fathers such as Justin, Irenaeus), dispensational premillennial (Walvoord), postmillennial (Edwards, Boettner) symmetrically, each with its strongest exegetical case (ezēsan "came to life" in 20:4-5; binding of Satan, Matt 12:29; 19→20 sequence). Say plainly that evangelical believers hold all of these and that the Lord's real, bodily return, the resurrection and the final judgment are common ground. Great white throne: judged by works, saved by the book of life (Eph 2:8-10). Do not end on the keys of Death and Hades.
  - 21-22: new creation as renewal, not escape (Rom 8:19-23; 2 Pet 3:13 with its debated "burned up"/"exposed" variant, cf. 2 Peter chapter); New Jerusalem as the people and a place, temple-city, cube = holy of holies (1 Kgs 6:20); 22:18-19 warning (not about the closed canon of the whole Bible as such, though many apply it so — hedge); 22:20 "Come, Lord Jesus". Do not end 22 on the morning star (22:16 is 00's ending).

## Meditation assignments (fresh; not in WORKFLOW's used lists)
Each meditation: an honest scenario from Chinese church/family life, gospel ground before any step, a word for the reader who has failed, ending on Christ and grace. Vary shapes (some end in adoration, some on one step, some in a short prayer — not all). Avoid "the one who said it may have been me" openings, "came near" endings, door endings, hand endings, the keys of Death and Hades, the morning star, and the "So I can... / If I have already..." two-paragraph close.
- 06: hiding (6:15-17 people calling on rocks to hide them "from the wrath of the Lamb"; Gen 3:8): a man hiding a gambling debt / business failure from his family; "who can stand?" (6:17). End on the Lamb himself as the only hiding place, because he bore the wrath (Isa 53:5 is used elsewhere: prefer 2 Cor 5:21 or Gal 3:13 — check WORKFLOW used lists first).
- 07: grief: an elderly widow or a migrant worker's family after a death; 7:15-17 the Lamb as shepherd. End on 7:17 "God will wipe away every tear from their eyes" (21 must not use tears).
- 08: 8:3-4 the prayers of all the saints rising with incense, after the silence (8:1): a shy believer who never prays aloud at the prayer meeting and thinks her prayers are small. End on prayers made acceptable at the golden altar before the throne, by Christ's mediation (not Heb 7:25, used in Hebrews 05).
- 09: 9:20-21 "they did not repent": a health scare that changed nothing; warnings ignored; my own hardening. End on God's patience as mercy — "I gave her time to repent" (2:21) / Rom 2:4 — and Christ still calling today.
- 10: 10:9-10 the little scroll sweet in the mouth, bitter in the stomach: a Sunday-school teacher or small-group leader for whom God's word has become bitter work; 10:11 "you must again prophesy" — recommissioned. End on the angel's oath, "there would be no more delay… the mystery of God would be fulfilled" (10:6-7).
- 11: faithful witness that looks defeated (11:7-12): a believer mocked by colleagues / a church whose witness seems to have died. End on 11:15 "the kingdom of the world has become the kingdom of our Lord and of his Christ".
- 12: the accuser (12:10): night-time accusation over past sin, abortion or a broken marriage (handle with care); conquered "by the blood of the Lamb" (12:11 is already alluded to in 03: build on 12:10 "thrown down" rather). End on the accuser thrown down and "Now the salvation… has come".
- 13: the mark and pressure to conform (13:16-17): a job, a licence or a promotion that requires a compromise; fascination with 666 instead of faithfulness. End on Christ who refused the kingdoms of the world rather than bow (Matt 4:8-10), and so gives the kingdom to those who bowed and repent.
- 14: 14:13 "Blessed are the dead who die in the Lord… their deeds follow them": an obscure faithful old believer (a deaconess who cleaned the church for 40 years) and the reader who thinks their life is wasted. End on "rest from their labors".
- 15: 15:3-4 the song of Moses and the Lamb sung by those who conquered, beside the sea of glass: worship after deliverance; a choir member who cannot sing after a loss; Exodus 15. End in adoration: "you alone are holy… all nations will come and worship you".
- 16: embarrassment at God's wrath (16:5-7 "Just are you… true and just are your judgments"): a modern reader, a university student, a pastor who never preaches judgment. End on Christ who drank the cup (John 18:11; Mark 14:36), so that the bowls are not poured on those in him.
- 17: the allure of Babylon's wealth and luxury (17:4; 18:3): a family's dream of a house, status, investment schemes. End on 17:14 "those with him are called and chosen and faithful" — being with the Lamb.
- 18: 18:11-13 the cargo list ending in "human souls": a workplace that treats people as cargo (996 culture, labour exploitation), and my own complicity; 18:4 "Come out of her, my people". End on God calling "my people" — people Babylon counted as cargo, he calls his own (avoid 5:9 "ransomed", used in Titus).
- 19: 19:7-9 the marriage supper and the "fine linen, bright and pure" that was "granted" her: feeling not good enough for the feast (not singleness / 催婚, used in 1 Cor). End on the Rider called Faithful and True (19:11).
- 20: fear of the great white throne and the books (20:12): an anxious believer who cannot shake fear of judgment. End on the book of life — "the Lamb's book of life" (21:27) — names written by grace (not on the keys of Death and Hades, not on 3:5).
- 21: 21:5 "Behold, I am making all things new": a disabled body, a damaged family, a ruined hometown — renewal, not escape. Do not use tears (07). End on the Lamb as the lamp of the city, "there will be no night there" (21:23-25).
- 22: the double "Come" (22:17, 20): the invitation to the thirsty and the church's prayer "Come, Lord Jesus"; 22:4 "they will see his face". Closing the whole Book Study series' last chapter. End on 22:21 "The grace of the Lord Jesus be with all."
